## What the evidence actually rules out

1. **The system has repeatedly predicted something smaller than its bill.** The historical opening gap direction beat a coin flip by ₹326 per trade and made ₹183 before *additional* slippage, with modeled execution and fees already included. The stressed version lost ₹30 and the 21-session real-quote comparison lost ₹257 at ₹205. The same-clock opening premium study measured about ₹80–130 of extra normalization per leg versus the later clock. These are two ways the same-time trade can have little value left. Do not add them again to a P&L that already contains the price change. A forecast improves the strategy only if the remaining movement after the actual entry can pay. [Auction and premium results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn351/README.md)
2. **A large visible gain can coexist with a losing entry.** Across SN351's 8,488 ATM observations, 64.7% touched +₹300 and 43.7% touched +₹750 during the next hour. Those are after baseline charges but before the additional stresses, and the raw hit statistic does not impose the order-time stop. At ₹205 a net +₹300 needs a baseline mark of +₹505, and at ₹400 it needs +₹700. The ₹1,048 average is the maximum chosen after reading the path. The actual fixed 20-minute payoff was −₹89.5 without added stress and −₹295.1 at ₹205. For illustration only, a payoff with exactly +₹750 on wins and −₹3,000 on losses needs an 80% win rate to break even. A real bracket has timeouts and smaller losses or gains, so use its whole payoff distribution. SN161 already tested wage locks; use this slot to audit the source of executable return, with one fixed exit, and avoid another grid of targets. 3. **The V8 receipt is encouraging for the trades it can price.** Nine of ten modeled entries found full size at the limit. Seven had usable archive coverage through the paper exit and two did not. The coverage file still records one stuck exit among the seven, and the runner values its unfilled remainder at the last bid. Summing only the six fully closed modeled positions gives **₹1,15,088 versus ₹92,270** for the house replay at ₹205 (rounded). That small complete subset supports continuing the shadow. The audit gives little reason to rebuild Gold’s signal to save execution costs. ### SN367 — Account for the difference between a peak and a paycheck

Using the fixed SN351 clocks and old wage controls, audit one net ₹750 bracket with the current stop and a fixed deadline. Pre-register whether exit trigger and actual fill share a packet or require a later quote, use the later eligible fill as the primary rule, and keep all four outcomes: target, stop, timeout and unresolved. Report the realized magnitude and frequency of each. Compare the same outcomes with cash and a same-clock/random-side arm. Include the largest actual bid before and after confirmation, and before/after the stop, as diagnostics. This identifies whether a candidate is late, loses too much on failures, or is selecting profitable moments from many unprofitable attempts. A familiar target/hindsight result does not become a new candidate.

### SN368 — Test if the option wings move before the future

Use the recorded NIFTY chain to construct fixed-delta put-versus-call skew separately for the front and next expiry. Remove contemporaneous futures changes and common ATM IV changes. Test whether a fresh, persistent same-direction change across both tenors **while futures are still quiet** has independent information about the *future’s subsequent move*. Then express that move through a single affordable liquid option, whose quote may already make the forecast uneconomic. Use fixed time and DTE strata, synchronous quotes with a minimum whole-lot depth, earlier-only calibration, an ATM-IV/futures-only control and matched quiet events. If there are too few reliable wings, or the effect is confined to the already repriced options without a later future move, close the study. US evidence on option skew motivates the measure, with longer horizons and different instruments. Never infer dealer inventory from unsigned OI. [Xing, Zhang and Zhao](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/abs/what-does-the-individual-option-volatility-smirk-tell-us-about-future-equity-returns/ECFD16BA9ACBDC8D577D1BD866FBEA72) · [Quote shape constraints](https://swh.princeton.edu/~yacine/cnvx.pdf)

### SN369 — Give the system an independent domestic tape

Take a narrow evidence snapshot of pre-authorized, timestamped NIFTYBEES quote/trade data if obtainable and align it with NIFTY futures and the cash index. Look for ETF-led flow that remains after adjusting for the contemporaneous underlying, declared tracking/carry basis and spread. Require the ETF observation to be new on a receipt clock, and the future to still be inside its pre-event spread/threshold, then measure actual future and option response. Compare future-only, ETF incremental, and time-shift/placebo arms, with price discovery time distinguished from stale index publication. ETF data is a sensor. The exchange page illustrates that iNAV can be missing on an observation, so carry missing status. Historical US evidence finds that who leads depends on the instrument. With no timestamped evidence mark feasibility only. [NSE ETF data fields](https://www.nseindia.com/market-data/exchange-traded-funds-etf) · [Hasbrouck’s ETF/futures study](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1540-6261.2003.00609.x)

### SN370 — Test a scheduled information arrival inside the Indian session

Start with the Hong Kong cash market resumption at 13:00 HKT, 10:30 IST on applicable days. The research hypothesis is that fresh cash repricing, *relative to the last already-known pre-resumption level or live reference*, can add information while NIFTY is still quiet. Require actual synchronized foreign quotes, carry the dated holidays and special sessions, and compare cash days with the same Indian clock when HKEX is closed plus clock-shift controls. Never label the whole lunch price change as news at the reopen. Check that lead survives at the first affordable Indian ask and predefined exit. London is a later replication test with its correct daylight-saving calendar, not a second scan for a best clock. This is an intraday test with a potentially frequent clock, in contrast to a small RBI event sample. [HKEX trading hours](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Global/Exchange/FAQ/Securities-Market?sc_lang=en) · [LSE trading hours](https://www.londonstockexchange.com/en/resources/faqs/personal-investing)

### SN371 — Test whether the opening forecast outlives the premium reset

Freeze 10:15 as an entry decision, with the 09:16 auction direction still fixed and the post-open move consumed so far measured causally. Ask whether the original directional effect remains beyond 10:15 on days where the matching option’s common IV component has normalized versus its earlier baseline. Compare this with a gap-only entry at 10:15, matched own-clock days, and the same path’s earlier entry. Match on the already traveled distance and debit. Decompose after-10:15 futures return versus option decay and IV and enforce a price cap. This is the cheapest immediate gap experiment using existing auction and option evidence. A cheaper premium alone gives no reason to trade if the later return is gone. Reuse the frozen form prospectively; treat seen samples as development. ### SN372 — Observe expiry pressure while buying the following contract

On dated NIFTY expiry mornings, when Gold cannot enter, use contemporaneous changes in the expiring chain around a fixed observed strike and volume/depth, paired with the next-week chain and the futures mid. Define the event from observed price/quote updates rather than assuming OI identifies a dealer side. Ask whether a front-expiry imbalance resolving leads a subsequent underlying move *after it is visible*, and whether a one-lot next-week option earns it at its actual quotes. Compare the same mechanism on non-expiry mornings, same direction near strikes with no imbalance, and cash. Confirm real next-week option coverage and use the early cut. This explicitly tests a different contract and source of information than the older late-day expiry book. ### SN373 — Measure what can actually be saved by waiting at a limit

For an independent signal with positive pre-stress room, compare crossing now versus a single short-lived resting one-lot limit with a fixed cancellation and no chase. Only score where a later trade or crossed book at acceptable levels is observable after arrival. Keep a conservative fill bound, a more permissive diagnostic bound, and no-fill outcomes. Report adverse selection by following the same side after fills and after misses, including the profitable moves where the bid order never executes. If the candidate only works under a touch assumption or cannot clear cost even at the optimistic bound, retire it. Apply this as a paired execution comparison, never as permission to reduce frozen stress. [Execution timing research](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa010) · [Retail limit-order research](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5979974)

### SN374 — Find the withdrawal policy that survives bad starts at the current cash

Run the cash-conserving journal from independently chosen start months at the same ₹30k trading and ₹30k spare. Compare fixed monthly, realized-profit-only and low-base-plus-variable withdrawals, with identical trade signals, 20% risk, deposit cap and funded growth. Simulate correlated bad intervals by replaying full contiguous blocks and show binding weeks and growth delays. Report monthly median, periods with zero payable cash and which payments would rely on opening principal. Compute the extra after-cost gap-day net needed to close the ₹750/session target without touching required Gold cash. This is a wage reliability result, distinct from alpha.
