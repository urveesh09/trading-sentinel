## What survived the audit

- **SN286, holiday calls.** The calendar repair removed the Saturday errors and improved the reported standalone development result. The four samples remain positive as run, and three turn negative without their best day. The correct distinction in the wallet is **₹18,016 direct call P&L** on 33 calls, **−₹741 direct without its best day**, and **₹18,942 change in combined account net** (₹869,825 versus ₹850,883). The last number incorporates the portfolio path. It takes a fresh replay to know the account delta after removing a winning day. The `gl.opx(..., 15:10)` check can accept a prior close within its lookback, and the wallet branch lacks the strict deadline check. I tested a missing-15:10 fixture, then checked the actual caches and found that all 66 standalone scored ITM sessions did have a 15:10 option bar. The presence of a bar supports a minute-price proxy. Executable bid and quantity are still separate evidence. Historical dates outside the verified calendar also remain inferred from observed index sessions. Keep the specified Dec 24 paper observation as a low-frequency ledger, with an exact exit receipt or unresolved status. [SN286 results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/results-sn286.json) · [SN286 implementation](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/sn286.py)
- **SN287, partner loss reduction.** Later trade episodes accounted for 145% of aggregate adverse change in the four alert sessions because existing exposure partly offset the decline. Removing later episodes helped three of four times, while immediately closing everything helped twice. Directionally, this favors an advisory like “Avoid opening new trades today. Manage existing positions using your exits.” The script reconstructs the alert rather than joining actual delivery and uses whole-trip marks. Additions within an already-open episode can be classified with open inventory. It also carries bids and does not separately reconcile costs. Use order-by-order attribution before claiming that most of the measured benefit is causal. [SN287/288 implementation](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/sn287_288.py) · [Recorded results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/results-sn287-288.json)
- **SN288, partner timing.** The reported −0.22% versus +0.09% and −₹320 versus −₹281 per lot are the placement-minute-plus-two comparison, using actual average buy prices and archived top bids. The code uses `placed_at`, not actual fill timestamps, and does not implement the planned ±20% premium match. The +1-minute bucket still shows a gross difference (+0.08% versus −0.08%), measured on a different clock. The executable evidence supports withholding a copy or scalp candidate. It cannot resolve a precise 60-second or shorter edge. The 0.35% comparison is against the placement minute's snapshot ask, so it is a price comparison across clocks. [SN287/288 implementation](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/sn287_288.py) · [Plan](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn286/PLAN.md)
- **SN290, execution costs.** The receipt has **36 contributing sessions and 748 complete cases**. The one-lot cost comparison uses visible depth, midpoint changes and broker charges. It supports ₹90 CE, ₹100 PE and around ₹190 per straddle **in that sample of 15- and 60-minute holds**, with a ₹4–9 conservative spread model. The reported ₹311–413 average absolute movement for single legs comes from shifting **both** entry and exit one minute. Signed means range from **−₹53 to +₹16**, and absolute straddle changes were ₹149–159. This is clock sensitivity on those fills. A live implementation needs separate entry and exit distributions around its actual triggers. Keep the frozen ₹205 and ₹400 stresses as separate scenario columns. ## What needs correction before a meaningful forward verdict

| Severity | Verified issue | Effect and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | `fwd.py` treats a failed Gold query as `gap_net is None`, which satisfies the pass test. A successful query uses only dates in `sn_gold_positions`, so a missing Gold day looks flat. The candidate can also beat an empty control. | A synthetic 20-session, 40-trade fixture returned `PASS` with unknown Gold status and zero control fills. Require `sn_gold_days` close records reconciled to positions, a known gap denominator with observable candidate coverage and a valid control. Treat unavailable data as insufficient. |
| **Critical** | `fw.hold` accepts any positive bid without checking size. It closes on the last packet if the requested exit was never reached, or at any later packet with a positive bid. | A synthetic 65-unit exit used a bid with **zero quantity**, booked the last tick **40 seconds early** when the deadline was unobserved, and treated a bid at **600 seconds** as the planned **60-second** exit. These cases can change economic results. Require executable depth and a bounded exit window. Put unresolved exposure on the capital ledger. |
| **High** | The collector processes any copied folder once. It checks neither completion nor the manifest file list, SHA-256 or bytes. An interrupted or early copy can persist indefinitely. `qualify` records selected events as text without making them part of the quality gate. | Collect closed sessions to a temporary directory, verify the manifest and coverage, then publish atomically. Track rejects and retry incomplete copies without resetting the recorded results. `complete` alone is insufficient because the recorder can reach a storage cap or restart. |
| **High** | The primary scorer is a signal study. SN291 accepts up to ₹15,000 in premium, while SN293 has no comparable premium cap. SN299 requires full debit plus fees within ₹3,000 of available risk. The per-trade stop can slip, and repeated losses do not consume a shared daily budget in `fw.py`. | Report the reason for every wallet rejection. At a 65-unit lot, ₹3,000 buys an ask of at most **46.15 points before fees**. A ₹15,000 premium ceiling together with a ₹3,000 stop is a different experiment. A passing signal with little affordable coverage has limited value for Gold. |
| **High** | SN293 uses receipt age for the target while training and peer samples can carry stale observations. Neither arm protects the decision price with a maximum ask. | A new packet with a repeated book can have a fresh receipt. A lag may disappear before the buy. Carry exchange and receipt timestamps, validate both ends of the move on the future, target and peers, and apply a predeclared price cap at entry. Treat same-frame packets as simultaneous. |
| **Medium** | SN291 compares raw totals against all impulses at a different trade frequency. SN293's most-repriced strike can have different sensitivity, premium and fill rates. The SN296 code covers SN291 only, uses a 300-second cap versus 60 seconds and has no independent busy state for its longer holds. | Publish original diagnostics under their actual definitions. Use paired and frequency-matched controls for conclusions about incremental signal value. Isolate duration from exit information and simulate occupancy independently. |
