## Verified preflight observations

- **Historical decision label.** The stored index close labeled 09:16 matched the final second of the 09:16 minute on nine of the first ten available sampled dates from January 16, 2026, and matched the final second of 09:15 on zero. SN394 prices and selects its ATM straddle with bar closes, so the historical result's “09:16” label cannot establish parity with the live 09:16:00 snapshot. The fixed label changed on five of 125 when both clocks were aligned to the previous completed minute, keeping the original nominal IV calculation times. This check used the existing Dev cache and sealed January–April second archive. It did not reprice any trades. ### SN397 — Determine what the “cheap open” actually detects

On a fixed cohort, make a four-cell comparison of cheap versus other mornings, each with auction direction and the same option budget on the opposite side. Match or stratify by expiry distance, weekends/holidays, auction source and gap magnitude. Report signed forward movement and a same-direction long option's realized P&L after removing the approximate effects of underlying move, time decay and costs. Record starting volatility level and time value so an unchanged but expensive option is visible. Separate differences in directional follow-through, option repricing and affordable fill rate. Control blocks of trading dates and report the full tested rule family. A robust cheaper-option explanation should remain visible after holding direction and contract characteristics comparable. [Muravyev and Ni on time-of-day volatility and option returns](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X19302193)
