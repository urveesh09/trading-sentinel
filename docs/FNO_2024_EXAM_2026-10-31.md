## The verdict

1. **The pick from the two earlier years failed.** TPB (the trend-day pullback) with the skip rule, at ₹5,000 risk with no lot limit and no brakes, lost **₹60,581** on ₹2.5 lakh in 2024, with a **38.6%** fall.
2. **"Good in both earlier years" did not carry into the third.** Only 1 of the 14 strategies positive in both 2025 and 2026 was positive in 2024. TPB alone lost ₹26,084 per lot and hit its own −15% stop in August.
3. **One rule passed: skip entries that are already stretched.** It beat the same book without it on all three books tested, and it also lifted random entries. It is the first idea from this research to pass a test on a year it never saw.
4. **Today's live book did better than expected, and adding the rule makes it the best candidate.** The four strategies with the rule are the only book tested here that stayed positive in all three years after the assumed extra cost, and only just: **+₹8,436 / +₹8,369 / +₹42,324** per lot.
5. **Today's limits are the right risk level.** Over three years they beat every bigger level taken to the exam.
6. **It is still not a daily income, and still not proven.** After the assumed cost, taking out each year's single best day leaves about ₹2,000 per lot over three years.

## 3. Why 2024 went this way

- **The trend filter sat out most of the year.** NIFTY's daily ADX stayed under 25 from February to late September, so the gate was open on only 57 of 248 sessions. The live book made 113 trades on 46 days, and **+₹18,514 of its +₹21,762 came in January**.
- **The filter earned its place.** The same four strategies with no filter lost ₹33,204. Across the three years the filter added +₹54,966, −₹26,869 and +₹83,603 per lot.
- **Strategies with no filter suffered in the quiet months.** TPB alone reached its −15% stop on Aug 19 and did not trade again. `ROBUST_CORE` stopped on June 21.
- **Past rank said very little.** Across 116 strategies, the rank correlation between 2025–26 profit and 2024 profit was **0.15**. Only 19 of 116 were positive on 2024, against 5 of 20 random books. The median strategy lost more than the median random book (−₹22,209 against −₹7,035).
- **2024's winner is a warning.** The best strategy on 2024 made +₹1,45,039 per lot, then lost ₹30,935 and ₹39,121 in the other two years. It must not be picked up.
- **Almost nothing survives all three years.** Only 2 of 116 strategies were positive in all three: today's live book, and one that made a trivial +₹418 / +₹1,777 / +₹5,833. None of the 20 random books did.

## 4. Is it generally profitable?

| Biggest fall | ₹22,252 | ₹26,852 |

- **It does not trade most weeks.** 99 of 148 weeks had no trade. Of the rest, 25 were up and 24 down.
- **A few big days pay for it.** Each year's single best day adds up to ₹58,686 of the ₹1,05,729. After cost and without those three days, what is left is **+₹2,043** over three years.
- **Plainly:** it looks like a small edge that collects on rare big trend days and roughly holds its money otherwise. That is not the steady daily income in the original vision. It is also not proof, because the rule was found by looking at 2025 and 2026, and only one unseen year has tested it.

## 5. Which risk level?

Last night's answer needs correcting. On 2025 and 2026, ₹5,000 risk with **no lot cap and no brakes** came out as the balanced choice, with today's limits second. With 2024 added, **today's limits win**: +₹45,331 against +₹32,182 on TPB + rule, with less than half the worst fall (18.2% against 38.6%).

₹12,500 risk per trade, the level that made the most money on the earlier two years, is negative over the three (−₹13,402) with a 76.3% fall. The earlier conclusion stands: **the system is not too conservative, and more size does not rescue it.**

## 7. How this was checked

- **Data:** the source file matched the download agent's recorded hash. 2,637,777 rows over 248 sessions, with no negative volumes, no bad prices and no rows outside the window. Fingerprint committed before any replay. Dataset unchanged afterwards.
- **The replays:** every book covered all 248 sessions; no named signal changed within a bar; no trade entered outside 2024 or on an expiry day.
- **Independent reproduction:** the sheets ran separately with no shared files, and three books came out trade-for-trade identical between them.
- **Money:** `check_book` found 0 problems across sheet 3: charges, day cap, own cash, lots, extra cost.
- **Scoring:** `report_x.py` re-applies each sheet's rule and asserts it reproduces the stored verdict.
- **The odd finding:** seven months with no live-book trades looked like a bug. An independent ADX calculation matched the replay's to the digit, and the daily bars match known NIFTY closes (June 4, 2024: 21,884.5). It is real.
- **Half-days (Mar 2, May 18):** futures exist only 09:15–09:59 and 11:30–12:30, so nothing can trade in the break or after the close. Only 1 distinct trade among the frozen books was left open at the end of a half-day, worth −₹192 per lot. **Every verdict is the same with those days removed,** or with that trade closed at the last price.
- **The invalid NIFTY BANK bar (Apr 19, 2024):** no scored book reads NIFTY BANK. It was left as it is, not filled.

**What these replays still cannot show:** real slippage (₹200 per trade is an assumption, and candles flatter fills by about ₹100–200 per lot), and the lot, because 65 is applied in all three years so results are comparable, though the real 2024 lot was different.
