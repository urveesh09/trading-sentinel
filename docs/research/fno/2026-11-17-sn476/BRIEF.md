## What Gold has earned

The strategy takes selective NIFTY entries from the N11 paper stream, applies its 0.85 stand-aside gate, buys an affordable in-the-money option when possible, and adds after a favorable 1-ATR futures move if cash and stop room allow. An efficiency breakout with a retest (EFF) is the clearest component to protect. Removing it hurt both historical periods in SN423. SN421 found that removing the ITM choice or reducing adds had a larger impact than small changes to the winner extension. Gold benefits from its entries and also takes more exposure when trends move in its favor. Those are separate contributions.
