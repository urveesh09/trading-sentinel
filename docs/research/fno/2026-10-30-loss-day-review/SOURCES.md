# Strategy literature: relevance, evidence and limits

Reviewed October 30, 2026. These are sources for mechanisms and experiment design, not imported profitability estimates. Published equity/futures results and US option mechanics do not establish a profitable NIFTY option book. No video, forum win rate, commercial backtest or social-media claim is used as proof.

## Primary sources reviewed

| Source | What it supports | Application to Sentinel / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [SMB Capital, Range Break Trading](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Technical-Analysis-Range-Break-Trading-Cheat-Sheet.pdf), two-page practitioner guide, full text | Describes initial range breaks, second-chance break/retest entries, and failed breaks returning inside a range. | Most direct blueprint for two distinct responses to extended/failed entries. It is educational practice, not a controlled NIFTY-options performance study. US-stock tick offsets and partial exits must not be transplanted into a one-lot NIFTY book. |
| [John Bollinger, Bollinger Band Rules](https://www.bollingerbands.com/bollinger-band-rules), creator's rules, full text | Touching a band alone is insufficient to identify a reversal; sustained trends can follow a band. | Supports requiring price rejection/confirmation for a range trade. The existing VWAP/RSI families' failures cannot be solved by replacing one overbought indicator with another. |
| [Schwab thinkorswim, TTM Squeeze](https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/reference/Tech-Indicators/studies-library/T-U/TTM-Squeeze.html), platform documentation | Compression is represented through the relationship between Bollinger and Keltner bands; momentum supplies direction. | A range can later release into expansion. A candidate should enter after observed expansion, account for failed releases, and keep session deadlines. Indicator documentation is not evidence of an edge. |
| [Heston, Korajczyk and Sadka, Intraday Patterns in the Cross-section of Stock Returns](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3535), authors' research abstract; Journal of Finance reference | Describes short-horizon reversal related to temporary liquidity imbalances and bid/ask bounce. | Economic rationale for confirmed mean reversion and careful execution. Cross-sectional US equities differ from a single index option; small apparent reversals can be consumed by spreads. |
| [Baltussen, Da, Lammers and Martens, Hedging demand and market intraday momentum](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf), Journal of Financial Economics 142 (2021), full text | Links late-session futures momentum to hedging demand and documents subsequent reversal. | Explains why continuation and reversal can both exist at different horizons. Its last-30-minute trade conflicts with Sentinel's 14:45 entry cutoff and 15:10 flattening; it is not a drop-in strategy. Public open interest does not by itself identify dealer positioning. |
| [OIC, Time Erosion vs. Delta Effect](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/leaps-time-erosion-versus-delta-effect), official education | Shorter-dated option time premium erodes as expiration approaches. | Motivates evaluating the executable option payoff, time and volatility alongside underlying direction. It does not identify the cause of our 23 direction-favorable losing trades: IV/Greeks attribution is still needed. |
| [Zerodha Varsity, Bull Call Spread](https://zerodha.com/varsity/chapter/bull-call-spread-2/) and [HKEX, Bull Call Spread](https://www.hkex.com.hk/eng/sorc/frontend/strategies_bullcall.htm), broker/exchange education | A moderate directional view can be expressed with a long lower-strike call and short higher-strike call; expiry profit is capped, with a net-debit loss bound under the complete spread payoff. | Candidate for a separate defined-risk study when outright premium is unsuitable. Both legs, actual quotes, fills, unwind and costs matter. This does not grant short-leg authority or establish affordability in Rs12,500. |
| [OIC, Long Call Butterfly](https://prd-web.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/long-call-butterfly), official education | A limited-payoff position benefits from the underlying finishing near its middle strike; time/volatility effects depend on location and remaining time. | Possible separate range-target experiment. Four option units and short legs, center-selection risk and execution costs make it inappropriate to assume a simple intraday cash-only replacement. Expiry payoff is not today's exit value. |
| [CME Group, 25 Proven Strategies](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/files/2022/25-proven-strategies.pdf), exchange payoff guide | Compares directional and volatility option structures and their exposure to time. | Useful payoff taxonomy. The title is not empirical proof. Long straddles pay two premiums and need sufficient movement; short straddles and futures introduce risk/margin beyond the present mandate. |
| [Zerodha, Hedged-position margins](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/margins/margin-leverage-and-product-and-order-types/articles/hedged-position-margins), official broker rules | Order sequence affects the margin needed for short options/futures and a hedge can reduce it. | Defined risk does not mean permission to trade a short leg or zero execution margin. No spread is assumed eligible for Monday without an explicit funded-margin and execution assessment. |

## Additional primary research located, with access limits

These inform the research map only. Full-text access failed; no numerical profitability claim from them is adopted.

- [Holmberg, Lonnback and Lundstrom, Assessing the profitability of intraday opening range breakout strategies, Finance Research Letters 10 (2013)](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1521889/FULLTEXT01.pdf): institutional search metadata/abstract identifies an ORB and contraction/expansion study on crude-oil futures. Full PDF retrieval timed out.
- [Drechsler, Moreira and Savov, Liquidity and Volatility, NBER 27959](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27959): abstract describes liquidity provision's exposure to volatility shocks. Relevant warning against treating reversal/short-volatility as a free hedge; full page/PDF returned access errors.
- [Vaghela, Greeks-Aware Profitability Labeling for Intraday Nifty 50 Options Trading](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6625120): author-supplied abstract proposes incorporating time, volatility and costs in an options label. Full text blocked. A modelled label cannot replace actual historical executable fills.
- [Subhash S, Open Interest Repositioning around Intraday Price-structure Breaks: Evidence from NIFTY 50 Index Options](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7394780): abstract describes testing a price/OI heuristic. Full text blocked. This is not a licence to infer institutional intent from open interest or claim a validated entry rule.
- [Pillai, Trading the Volatility Risk Premium on Nifty 50: Strategy Backtest with Realistic Frictions](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6876580): author-supplied abstract discusses short-volatility backtests. Full text blocked; pricing, costs and tail-risk claims were not independently assessed. No short-volatility recommendation is made.

Linda Raschke's indexed volatility-breakout PDFs were also located, but their URLs returned 404. No detailed strategy rule is attributed to unavailable text. Generic strategy blogs, forum returns and unverified NIFTY backtests were excluded as evidence.

## Research conclusion from the literature

The literature supplies several plausible mechanisms: retest/continuation, rejection/reversal, compression/expansion, and option-structure selection. None tells us in advance that an entire day will lose, and none supplies a proven opposite trade for every loss. Our data determines the hypotheses; a prospective, costed, shared-book experiment must determine whether any actually improves Sentinel.
