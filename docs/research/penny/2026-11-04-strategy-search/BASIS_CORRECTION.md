# Nominal-price audit correction, before validation

The initial 153-symbol daily trials are exploratory and cannot establish an
actual Rs 1–55 historical universe result. Yahoo adjusts historical OHLC and
volume for subsequent stock splits/bonuses. Official NSE January 2, 2024 data
places PCJEWELLER at Rs 55.05 and volume 33,298,237; the archived Yahoo response
has Rs 5.505 and volume 332,982,370. The split ratio is 10:1. Selection at the
adjusted historical price therefore changes eligibility and whole-share sizing.

All original raw responses, freezes, trial results and failed 2019 NSE file
probe are retained. Do not use the original daily trials for a nominal-price
improvement claim. The intraday study is a separate recent-price experiment.

Primary daily comparison is repeated under `basis-v2/` on the **same 108-symbol
cohort for every arm**: exclude all 45 histories with reported split/bonus
events, plus any available Jan 2, 2024 NSE cross-check differing by over 0.5%.
No additional nominal-price mismatches were found in available cross-checks.
This is an availability/basis restriction applied without outcome selection,
not a reconstructed point-in-time universe. The 45 exclusions include major
current Penny names. No source price, volume or existing snapshot is repaired.

The restricted cohort still has survivorship, absent fundamentals, quote/depth,
daily close/auction execution and point-in-time membership limits. All results
are conditional historical research. A complete all-universe replay would need
dated identities, raw historical prices and corporate-action quantity/cash
mechanics; this study does not claim to supply them.

Primary cash stays identical between paired strategies. Native small-capital
checks and adverse slippage must accompany a promising large-book result.
Validation remains unopened at this correction. The reserved Jan–Jul Penny
exam and all F&O/expiry data remain untouched.
