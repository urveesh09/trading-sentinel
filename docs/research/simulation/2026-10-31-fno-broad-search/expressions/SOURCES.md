# Additional primary-source mechanics review

Read October31,2026. Parent SOURCES.md records the broader research review.
No published foreign-market result is treated as NIFTY performance evidence.

- [OCC/OIC strategy guide](https://www.optionseducation.org/getmedia/68305977-b772-41c8-bf1d-3d405725b3cf/options-strategies-quick-guide-2025.pdf):
  definitions/payoffs for bounded debit, credit and iron structures.
- [CME long-call scenarios](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/long-call-scenarios):
  underlying direction and implied volatility both influence premium. Correct
  direction alone does not establish a profitable option entry/exit. This
  study does not isolate historical theta/IV contributions from its candles.
- [CME volatility and strategy alignment](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/files/options-aligning-volatility-with-strategies.pdf):
  volatility outlook matters to payoff selection; no NIFTY return imported.
- [Cboe VIX overview](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/):
  US index volatility-premium context motivates a separate credit hypothesis;
  it does not establish a comparable exploitable premium in these NIFTY data.
- [Fidelity iron-condor mechanics](https://www.fidelity.com/viewpoints/active-investor/iron-condor-strategy):
  four legs add transaction costs; payoff bounds do not make a profitable
  entry or ensure simultaneous execution.

The original inverse-credit vertical opposes the original option forecast.
The extension explicitly tests SAME-direction credits using the other option
type, plus neutral structures. They use recorded single-leg clocks solely for
paired expression comparison. An independently timed volatility strategy could
behave differently and requires its own joint-leg admission/management adapter.
Terminal payoff bounds are not historical broker margin proof, cash-settlement
evidence, guaranteed stop prices, or permission to sell uncovered options.
