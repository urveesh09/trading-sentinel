# Erratum to round 6 (EN56–EN70), November 18, 2026

This erratum follows the independent audit in `docs/research/simulation/2026-11-18-expiry-review-en71/AUDIT.md`. I checked each finding against the frozen code and agree with all four.

The original files stay unchanged: `results6.json`, `ANATOMY6.md`, `pk6.py`, `post6-en58.json` and the freeze. Every corrected number below comes from new, separately versioned code. It is research only, in Dev. No strategy, Production file or forward registration changes, and EN55's registered forward comparison is untouched.

## F1. A fresh quote can carry an old last trade

**What was wrong.** EN58's price-through fill estimator (`pk6.rest`, `'through'`) accepted a later packet's last-traded price as proof that our resting buy had filled. It never checked that the trade happened after our order arrived, or that any new volume traded. The packet decoder already carries the last-trade time, so the check was possible.

**How it is fixed.** `docs/research/simulation/2026-11-18-expiry-en71/pk7.py` adds estimator version 2. Every fill now needs evidence from after arrival, and the result is three bounds rather than one rate:

| Bound | When it counts as a fill |
|---|---|
| **LOWER** (definite) | A trade strictly below our price, after arrival, with new volume. Or a fresh best ask strictly below our price. |
| **QUEUE** (estimate) | Post-arrival volume at or below our price that passes the displayed queue ahead of us. Whole lots; partial fills allowed. |
| **UPPER** (possible) | Any post-arrival trade at or below our price, or any fresh ask at or below it. |

- Last-trade stamps have whole-second precision. A trade counts as definitely after arrival only if its whole second is at or after arrival.
- Fills that land during the cancellation acknowledgement are labelled `PASSIVE_RACE`.
- Synthetic checks pass. The audit's stale-trade case (an unchanged ₹9.90 last trade from 60 seconds earlier, no new volume) fills under version 1 and under none of the version-2 bounds.
- Version 1 reproduces exactly from the frozen code.

**What changes (Nov 17, `erratum-en58-v2.json`).** These are all modelled fills on one recorded session, not exchange executions.

- **P0.** The fill count does not change under any bound. On cheap expiry options, a genuine post-arrival trade-through happens within the 60-second rest.
  - The four unsupported witnesses the audit found all fill later on post-arrival evidence.

## F2. The 49.5% "direction right" figure was 50% by construction

EN56's opportunity library lists both a call and a put at each grid time. Its "underlying favourable" test applies no forecast.

- On NIFTY, 89.3% of the filled rows (22,704 of 25,425) sit in matched opposite-side pairs with the same time, offset and exit. One side's favourable move is the other side's unfavourable one.
- So 49.5% measures nothing about forecasting.

**Still correct:**
- The sampled options lost money on average (−₹79.48 at ₹2,500 and 8 minutes).
- 40.4% of favourably signed moves still lost on the option.

**Withdrawn:** the reading "direction is right only 49.5% of the time". The relevant forecast measure is one causal forecast per clock, the E03-time/side comparison, which round 7's EN71 now reports.

## F3. Live paper cannot confirm a resting-order fill

The round-6 wording "only real resting orders on paper can test EN55's patient entry" was wrong. A paper order never enters the exchange queue.

- Live paper sessions can test timing, reconnects, deadlines and a declared fill estimator.
- Their passive fills stay modelled, now with version 2's bounds.
- Only actual exchange executions establish actual fills. No cash experiment is proposed or authorised here.

## F4. Narrower statements

- **EN60.** Its NIFTY result (−₹9,777) beat the median of its random-side null (−₹13,885) while still losing. The FAIL verdict stands. The phrase "every new family lost against random" is withdrawn.
- **EN61.** Its reported log losses (model 0.985, intercept 1.046) were measured on the calibration sample used to fit its temperature. They are not an out-of-sample forecast score. The forward-day P&L still lost.
- **EN69.** The decomposition is bookkeeping under its model and ordering, and the published table omitted the execution term. The full reconciliation for NIFTY E03 puts:

  - This supports directional dominance in the modelled paths.
  - It does not prove that no structural put premium exists.
- **EN68.**
  - The halt shares come from a resampling model of seen sessions, not from measured probabilities for next year.
  - The phrase "about one year in three for every book" is withdrawn. The table itself shows SENSEX EN55 at 43%, equal-risk E03 at 65% and ACTIVE NIFTY at 83%.
- **EN70.** A median volume ratio of 1 does not mean every bar matched. Identical decisions on one recorded afternoon cannot establish parity in general. The PARITY NOT ESTABLISHED verdict stands.
- **Statistics.** Round 6's bootstrap value was the tail mass of an uncentred i.i.d. expiry bootstrap. It is a confidence proxy, not a calibrated p-value. Round 7 replaces it with three things:
  - a sign-flip randomisation test on calendar-week blocks;
  - a separate block-bootstrap interval;
  - a calibration of the test under simulated nulls.
- **The verdicts in round 6 do not change.** No strategy passed then, and none passes on this correction.
