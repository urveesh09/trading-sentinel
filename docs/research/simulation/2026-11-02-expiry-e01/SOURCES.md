# Primary-source background, checked November 2, 2026

These explain why the mechanisms deserve testing; none supplies evidence that
E01 earns money in India. The archived native instrument/calendar identity and
the paired packet replays determine this experiment's eligibility and outcome.

- [NSE market timings](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings):
  check the applicable derivatives session, not the cash market's close.
- [NSE closing auction](https://www.nseindia.com/market-data/closing-auction-session):
  the auction changes execution assumptions. An index-option quote is not
  evidence that its sampled bid was executable as a broker order in that period.
- [NSE 2026 holidays](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays):
  October 20 is Dussehra; the study retains the actual October 19 NIFTY session
  instead of assuming every expiry must fall on its usual weekday.
- [BSE expiry-revision circular, exchange-hosted PDF](https://www.indiainx.com/circulars/20250718-1/20250718-1.pdf):
  Thursday expiry changes are dated rules. Never project today's weekday onto
  older data without dated contract evidence.
- [Cboe 0DTE resources](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/0dte):
  near-expiry option sensitivity makes premium loss and path/depth monitoring
  important. US examples are not a validated NIFTY/SENSEX strategy.
- [Cboe positioning and market-impact discussion](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact):
  aggregate options positioning is context, not a deterministic directional
  forecast. No invented gamma exposure or hindsight regime switch was added.

The engineering inference is narrow: test C's missing premium backstop and B's
whole-pair loss path separately, preserving upside rules, fees and recorded
depth. E01's 30% line came from A's already shipped policy before scoring, not
from choosing the best point of a parameter grid. The whole-pair hypothesis
failed its empirical test and stays rejected despite its plausible story.
