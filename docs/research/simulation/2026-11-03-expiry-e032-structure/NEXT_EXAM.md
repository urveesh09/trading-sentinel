## A separate microstructure question needs different evidence

Question: can **changes** in executable supply/demand, combined with futures
leadership, predict a move larger than fees, spread and latency before it is
already priced? The failed snapshot rule does not answer every faster rule.

Record, in Dev/paper through a separately implemented GitHub rollout:

- Current-expiry full five-level quotes for all causal candidate strikes,
  including held contracts; index and independent future stream.
- Exchange quote/trade timestamps, local wall and monotonic receive times,
  decision, simulated order-send and first eligible subsequent quote times;
  expiry master, lot/tick identity and changing universe memberships.
- Raw incremental observations, disconnects, sequence/gap markers, cumulative
  traded volume, spreads and sizes. Broker full-mode quotes remain a sampled
  view, not exchange-complete adds/cancels or an identified dealer inventory.
- No fill at a decision quote; marketable walks use only later observed depth
  after declared latency. Passive fills require conservative queue evidence;
  a price touch alone never fills. No invented fills during outage periods.

Keep E03.1 as contemporaneous control. Freeze at most two mechanisms before
reading future outcomes: a signed best-quote-change/price-leadership trigger,
and an execution-quality veto of E03.1 entries. Define normalization only from
prior observations, event eligibility and loss budgets in advance. Require an
estimated tradable move to clear the actual current all-in fee/spread hurdle;
positive midpoint prediction alone is insufficient.

Measure all real latency cases (100/500/1,000 ms and the actually observed delay)
without selecting whichever latency wins. Reject unavailable granularity.
Before portfolio scoring, reserve separate untouched expiry days and keep all
their outcomes sealed; compare full cash books with same daily premium limits,
drawdown brakes, outages, cooldown and open-position carry. A markout study is
not that portfolio. Forward count >=20 closed tickets is a review minimum,
not proof; require distributed after-cost improvement, ex-best positive and
no extra downside before any promotion. Never buy speed/increase size based
only on this report. No such evidence was acquired or scored here.
