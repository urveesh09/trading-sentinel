# Five-minute futures shock repair

## Plan before seeing signal frequencies

The partial 2022 failure included shorts that made little favorable progress despite a daily trend reading and entry below session reference prices. Test a separate contrarian opportunity at the moment a fresh intraday shock starts to reverse. The anchor is the pre-shock price on the same contract. The previous day's range, the opening gap and the native entry decision are outside this rule. Published inventory and liquidity work offers a mechanism for temporary price pressure. Its evidence comes from U.S. equities on longer horizons, so the NIFTY setup needs independent evidence. [Hendershott and Menkveld](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/hender/price_pressures.pdf), [Nagel](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653).

Use consecutive completed five-minute NIFTY front-future bars from 09:15 IST on ordinary sessions. Between 10:30 and 14:00 inclusive, flag the first pair in which the shock close moves at least three times the median absolute close-to-close change of the 12 preceding bars and its traded volume is at least twice the median of those bars. The shock must close within the outer third of its own range in the direction of the move. The next bar must close 20–40% of the shock distance back toward the pre-shock close and leave the shock extreme intact. Enter toward the pre-shock close. Place the stop one tick beyond the shock extreme and require at least 1.5 times the stop distance to the target. A missing or invalid observation before the first decision makes the session unscorable. Use one opportunity per session. Exclude dates matching the nearest option expiry or the front future expiry and use that future's own volume.

First count only futures signal inputs on the same frozen 2023–October 2026 development windows as the range reclaim study. Require 30 opportunities across the declared sample before building any executable options study. Report the count, its coverage and clocks. Keep the 2021 reclaim and 2022 N012-A holdouts separate. A qualifying count earns a frozen standalone forward experiment with actual execution evidence, cash and stress. This slice changes no runtime, settings, schema, broker operations or rollout. Rollback retires the isolated research rule.
