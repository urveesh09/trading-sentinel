The low-volatility gate comparison relies on realized volatility and a small pool of shadow outcomes. For a different source, acquire the NSE's official India VIX and NIFTY 50 daily history from January 2021 through November 4, 2026. India VIX estimates 30-calendar-day implied volatility from NIFTY option quotes. It is not an intraday option price.
