# Round 8. A new input, overnight cross-market transmission

Prior local direction conditions and extra premium capacity did not transfer. Test whether a large completed U.S. risk move can help distinguish two different Indian opening setups. The U.S. input is causal. Use Nasdaq Composite and Cboe VIX closing values with a date strictly before the Indian session, at most four calendar days old. Require at least 60 earlier common observations. Require absolute Nasdaq return at or above the 80th percentile of its 60 earlier daily absolute returns and at least 0.8%, with opposite VIX movement of at least 0.6 index points. No use of a U.S. close from the Indian trade's date. The selected data are development inputs. [NSE research paper on overnight spillover](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/research/Paper39.pdf), [FRED Nasdaq Composite](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NASDAQCOM), [FRED Cboe VIX](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS).

- TRANSMISSION adds a one-lot early continuation after aligned opening strength and a closed-bar retest of the opening boundary. Retest has a 0.25 ATR touch tolerance. Require current follow-through outside the opening range, still within 35% of opening range width. Target twice planned underlying risk.
- ABSORPTION looks for the opposite direction when India opened on the U.S. side by at least 25% of the prior India session's range and then crossed back through the previous close on two fully closed five-minute bars. Require a new crossing rather than repeatedly taking an old gap fade. Target twice planned underlying risk.

Use only contiguous opening bars, the same front futures contract for the previous close, and fully closed five-minute bars for decisions between 10:00 and 11:40. Each arm adds at most one trade per day while the existing book is flat and without a native signal. Ordinary N11T risk, stand-aside, fees, simulated bid/ask, exit hierarchy, prior outcomes and one-lot limit remain. Scope is regular F&O, with no use of announcement dates as trade labels. Discard a family that fails to transfer.
