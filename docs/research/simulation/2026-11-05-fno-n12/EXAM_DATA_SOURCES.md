## Alternatives checked from provider documentation

- **Groww Backtesting API.** The documentation lists contract-specific futures and options history from 2020, with OHLC, volume, F&O open interest and a 30-day range per one-minute call. It requires an authenticated plan with Backtesting access. Its sample timestamp has no offset, so the clock convention and the exact 2022 contracts still need a sample check. This is the closest documented alternate source for the one-minute NIFTY exam. [Backtesting endpoint](https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/curl/backtesting), [data access plans](https://groww.in/blog/api-trading-on-groww).
- **NSE Data & Analytics.** NSE lists a subscribed historical F&O trade and order product with trade time, instrument, expiry and strike fields. The trade ticks can be aggregated into minute OHLC and volume if the requested dates and exact contracts are supplied. Point-in-time OI needs its own verified source. The ordinary historical bhavcopy gives daily totals. [Historical data subscription](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/eod-historical-data-subscription), [F&O historical layout](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/press/Hist_Order_data_layout.pdf).
- **Other standard APIs.** Dhan's expired options endpoint is indexed by strike relative to spot. It does not establish continuous history for a fixed expired contract. Upstox documents six months of expiry discovery. Global Datafeeds lists three months of one-minute backfill for its standard NFO API. These documented routes lack the required 2022 fixed-contract coverage. [Dhan expired options](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/expired-options-data/), [Upstox expiry discovery](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-expiries/), [Global Datafeeds backfill](https://globaldatafeeds.in/global-datafeeds-apis/global-datafeeds-apis/introduction/type-of-data-available/).

## Alternate-source qualification

Use the approved guarded Breeze route first. If alternate access is already available or separately approved, test it on previously scored June–December 2022 sessions. Select an ordinary session, a front-future roll boundary and fixed CE and PE contracts with reported volume and OI. Establish the bar timestamp, 09:15–15:10 clock grid, price scale, volume units, missing-minute behavior and dated expiry identity. Compare the seen sample against the frozen inputs and reject an unexplained mismatch. Keep unknown OI distinct from zero and exclude synthetic fill prices.

Before any alternate source can enter the one-shot exam, freeze its exact provider, dates, requests, contract identities, coverage report and normalization. All arms must read the same prepared inputs. Keep any provider boundary visible and verify overlapping candles. The existing preparer is bound to the guarded Breeze metadata, so another provider needs a separate input-preparation contract while retaining the original candidates and pass rules. A successful candle fallback would support the registered minute exam. The exact quote and depth requirement for the two-venue control remains a separate evidence problem.
