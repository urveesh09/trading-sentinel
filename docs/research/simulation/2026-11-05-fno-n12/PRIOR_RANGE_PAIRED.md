# Paired reclaim quote comparison

## Plan before implementation

Reuse the two-venue paired quote replay's clocks, native planner and coverage checks. Compare N11T with N11T plus reclaim from separate ₹250,000 wallets on one qualified tape. Require an independently generated list of frozen reclaim signals, and match each one to its first verified quote or mark it unavailable. An empty list is an explicit no-signal assertion from the upstream futures coverage check. Preserve actual quote depth and apply native fees in both arms. Treat supplied spread cash and exposure as common external events. This first quote comparison exposes arithmetic and attribution. A separately frozen symmetric stress replay and pass rules are still required before economic qualification.

Report changed native entry decisions, exact native tickets and earned adds, and the native positions, premium, trade count, realised cash, brakes, stance and re-entry levels at each changed decision. Identify trades by strategy clock, venue and original native signal, rather than by each arm's independent position ID. Preserve changed exits and reconcile the aggregate difference to reclaim P&L plus the change in native P&L. Keep every missing scan, expected reclaim quote or held observation visible. Withhold both summaries on incomplete coverage. Keep the original N012-A entrypoint and 2021/2022 freezes intact.

Test quote/tape identity, omissions, missing bid after entry, unchanged empty-signal parity, changes in native lot size and a changed earned-add ticket. Use only synthetic executions. No scheduler, broker, configuration, migration or rollout. Rollback retires the reclaim pair and restores the previous private replay driver. Future work needs an independently qualified quote and spread feed, the standalone market verdict, and precommitted economic pass rules.

## Verified behavior and limits

`paired_reclaim.compare_reclaim` accepts the declared regular scan clocks, completed-bar signals and matching quote packets. It rejects duplicate or mismatched packets, and withholds the pair for a missing first quote or a quote outside the entry minute. Required held quotes, management cadence, exact bids, expiry-qualified chain identity and external spread inputs remain subject to the paired replay contract. The upstream tape builder must prove that the signal list is complete and that a quote is the first verified one. The replay itself cannot infer either from a missing event.

The receipts link each changed native initial ticket and earned add to its original signal, clock and venue. For changed entries the report includes the actual planner decisions, cash, held exposures, premium, trades, brakes, stance and re-entry levels in each wallet. A confirmed result reconciles `paired net difference = reclaim net + change in native net`. Common external spread P&L cancels in that equation. The native change includes any later cash and risk interactions with the supplied spread exposure.

Eight synthetic checks passed, including a reclaim stop that reduced the next native ticket and removed its earned add. A different fixture accepted identical native economics under different internal position IDs and attributed the entire difference to reclaim. Missing or late first quotes and a missing held bid withheld both summaries, including when the bid later returned. The wider reclaim, exam, shared-control and N012-A checks passed 89 tests. The atlas remains at 304 modules. The 2021 and remaining 2022 prices stayed sealed. This is Dev research with synthetic evidence and base quote fees. It records neither an economic verdict nor a symmetric doubled-cost replay.
