# RBI MPC calendar evidence

This calendar lists 27 realized regular RBI Monetary Policy Committee decision dates from June 2022 through October 2026. It was compiled from the RBI calendar and statement archive without market prices, quote coverage or outcomes. Special meetings, post-policy press conferences and subsequent minutes are outside scope.

The date and resolution URL come from the same dated row of the [official RBI monetary policy archive](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Annualpolicy.aspx). The advertised clock comes from RBI's description on the linked statement broadcast. An empty evidence cell is marked with a dash. The selected verified actual stream starts come from `liveBroadcastDetails.startTimestamp` on those first-party broadcast pages, converted from UTC to IST. The exact time of the first spoken decision and first publication of the written resolution are unverified. A fixed calendar window can use the date without claiming an exact release time.

## Advance schedule evidence

The RBI archive includes the annual notices below. Match the meeting's last date against the realized resolution date and check revisions before treating a date as known in advance.

| Fiscal year | RBI notice date | Archive year field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2022–23 | 2022-03-30 | 2023 |
| 2023–24 | 2023-03-24 | 2024 |
| 2024–25 | 2024-03-27 | 2025 |
| 2025–26 | 2025-03-26 | 2026 |
| 2026–27 | 2026-03-23 | 2027 |

- RBI's [July 21, 2022 revision](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=54069) moved the meeting from August 2–4 to August 3–5.
- RBI's [June 6, 2025 revision](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60612) moved the meeting from August 5–7 to August 4–6. The archive's [2025–26 schedule notice](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60062) has a separate press release ID.
- For 2026–27 see the [March 23, 2026 schedule notice](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=62422).

## Reproduction

Open the official archive and select each fiscal year from 2022–23 through 2026–27. For scripted retrieval, GET the archive and POST its hidden ASP.NET form fields with `hdnYear` set to the fiscal year end and `UsrFontCntr$btn` set to an empty string. In each dated Monetary Policy Statement block, capture the resolution's Full Document link and the RBI Governor address link. Preserve the dated schedule block and any amendments separately. Where available, the [YouTube videos resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos) exposes scheduled and actual stream start as separate fields. Preserve source snapshots and hashes when freezing the calendar.
