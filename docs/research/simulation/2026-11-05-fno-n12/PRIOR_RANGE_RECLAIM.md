# Prior-session range reclaim

## Research plan

A new standalone candidate tests a failed auction at a previous-session extreme in the same dated NIFTY future. Earlier entry studies used the current day's opening range or overnight gap. Round 22 showed that a losing add-on can block a profitable native trade even after the add-on closes. This rule first needs evidence as its own sleeve. Research on exchange-rate support and resistance motivates the hypothesis that a widely visible boundary can interrupt or accelerate a move. Transfer to NIFTY is an open question. [New York Fed support and resistance study](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/epr/00v06n2/0007osle.pdf), [stop-loss cascades study](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr150.html).

Use the prior observed regular session, at most four calendar days earlier, from 09:15 through 15:29 IST for the same dated future used today. Require all 375 valid minutes, and require today's 09:15 open strictly inside the prior high-low. Exclude the nearest option and futures expiry dates. Use only contiguous completed five-minute bars from today's 09:15 open. From 10:00 to 14:00 inclusive, look for three consecutive closes that go inside, outside one side, then strictly back inside. Require the reclaim to close past the midpoint of the swept bar's range in the direction of the proposed trade. Reject a sequence that also breaches the opposite prior boundary during the sweep or reclaim. Take the first qualified signal per session.

For a long, put the stop one NIFTY futures tick below the lowest low of the sweep and reclaim. For a short use one tick above their highest high. Use a fixed 0.05-point buffer in this first study. Risk from the reclaim close must be between 5% and 25% of the prior range. The midpoint of the prior range is the target and must offer at least 1.5 times that risk. The signal is stamped at the reclaim bar close. A later execution model must recheck the risk and reward at the first causal quote.

Before any P&L work, count first eligible signals from the frozen 2023–October 2026 development sessions. Read only prior-day and decision OHLC, preserve every exclusion and report only counts, dates, clocks and directions. Require at least 30 signals across these windows or retire the rule as written. Use exact frozen input hashes and test the pattern, missing clocks, threshold boundaries and timing. No P&L, current strategy settings, deployment or migration in this slice. Rollback retires the research rule. The frozen A test for January–May 2022 remains separate.

If the frequency gate passes, freeze a standalone execution contract before seeing any untouched outcome. Use one NIFTY option lot with the native strike, quote and premium-risk gates, dated contract evidence, a one-trade daily cap and a standalone risk ledger. Use the midpoint target, the structural stop, the native premium backstop and hard flat. Keep the existing ₹250,000 N11T comparison scale and declared cost stresses. Any shared-wallet proposal must later measure what it does to native re-entry, pause, position size and cash.
