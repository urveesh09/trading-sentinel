# Round 26. Midday rotation toward morning value

The previous tests tried opening reversals, delayed continuation, relative participation, volatility and alternate payoffs. Test a different source of direction. After a stretched morning, does a partial reversal around lunch carry far enough to buy an option? This first screen uses dated NIFTY futures moves and a fixed clock. It separates directional viability from fills, premium decay, lifecycle competition and margin.

Calculate morning value from the 09:15–12:29 volume-weighted futures minute closes. Require all morning minutes, positive total volume and a valid morning range. At 13:00 the 12:44 close must be at least 30 points and 55% of the morning range away from value. The 12:59 close must have recovered 15%–50% of that displacement, with the 12:54 close already on the recovery side of the 12:44 close. Use the sign toward morning value. Allow at most one signal per non-expiry session. Score the signed move from the positive-volume 13:01 close to the positive-volume 14:15 close. Require all completed input minutes through 12:59 and the two exact marks. No substitute for missing clocks. Use the dated nearest future and option calendars. Split the five historical windows and preserve the November 5 observation as a separate case.

Report signals, missing data, mean/median signed points, positive years, and the signed points left after debiting a fixed 15-point hurdle per trade and excluding the five largest contributions. The hurdle is a deliberately coarse price-viability prerequisite. It makes no option return claim. Require at least 20 priced signals, at least four windows with positive hurdle-adjusted points, positive aggregate beyond the strongest five and no missing outcome among otherwise eligible signals before pricing options. If the screen fails, retire this specification without tuning its cutoffs on these results. No runtime, order, rollout or settings changes. Rollback is to retire the study.
