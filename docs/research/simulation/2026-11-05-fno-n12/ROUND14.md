# Round 14. SENSEX confirmation as a different information source

Measure whether the lagging NIFTY future catches up when SENSEX makes a directional opening move. Use the existing sealed 2025–2026 SENSEX tape and NIFTY development tapes. Restrict to the SENSEX source calendar span and to a NIFTY option expiry strictly after the session. Both monthly futures must be selected from available dated contracts and must not expire that day. Require all exact minute bars from 09:15 through 09:59 in both and the 10:01 and 10:44 closes. Report missing clocks before scoring. Do not apply this comparison to 2022–2024 without a matching SENSEX source.

At 10:00 the opening direction is the sign of the SENSEX move from the 09:15 open to the 09:59 close. Require a displacement of at least 60% of its opening high-low range and a NIFTY move in the same direction of at most 30% of its own opening range. Skip if the observed native N11T book already opened a position by 10:00. Measure the signed movement of both futures from the 10:01 close to the 10:44 close. Also record the change in normalized lead and whether a later N11T entry would compete with the candidate interval. The admission rule never looks at that future competition.

This is a coverage and signal screen. There are no option prices, fills or estimated profits. Test a complete option lifecycle only if both windows contain at least 20 signals, the median signed NIFTY move is positive in both and their aggregate signed NIFTY move remains positive after removing the five best dates and subtracting five index points per remaining signal. Five points is a screening hurdle and is not an execution-cost conversion. All available periods remain development. No settings, data calls, deployment, strategy or cash allocation change.
