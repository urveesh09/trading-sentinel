# Round 25. Futures instead of option premium on existing signals

The strategy often has cash available. Test whether a different contract payoff can use that cash without widening planned loss. Start with a fixed-admission economic diagnostic of one dated front NIFTY future at the native N11T entry and exit clocks. This preserves market direction and holding time and isolates changes in exposure, option decay, spread and turnover taxes. Native option-specific reasons still determine the historical exit clocks. A favorable result would need a separate, causally executable paper lifecycle before it could count toward N012.

Use a normalized lot of 65 units to match the previous paper comparisons. Require exact positive-volume futures marks at both archived clocks and parity to the native underlying marks. Use the current futures schedule on the full historical tape, consistent with applying current option charges to the controls. Include the combined NSE/IPFT charge, SEBI fee, GST, stamp duty on the buy and STT on the sell, even when a sell opens a short. Use two index points of adverse execution on each side and four points per side for sensitivity. Also report the zero-execution-cost gross result as context. The modeled costs and one-minute prints supply no executable quote. [Brokerage and levies](https://zerodha.com/charges/), [NSE transaction charges](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA73061.pdf), [NSE STT change](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FATAX73524.pdf).

The selective case can replace an initially one-lot option order only if there is no native open position at its entry clock and the original underlying stop, current fees and four points of adverse execution per side put the estimated loss within ₹2,500. Count 13% of futures notional as a research margin proxy and require it, a ₹25,000 liquidity buffer and that estimated loss to fit the ₹250,000 starting wallet. Show 10% and 16% proxy sensitivity. These percentages are scenarios. A margin-verified paper policy would require exact contract SPAN and ELM observed before entry. A futures stop can slip beyond its planned loss. Keep futures out of the ₹15,000 experiment. [NSE Clearing margins](https://www.nseclearing.in/risk-management/equity-derivatives/margins).

Freeze the diagnostic before reading paired economics. Report coverage, modeled changes by year, worst incremental date and the result after dropping the top five changes. Preserve native P&L for the comparison's unselected rows, and report missing observations separately. A complete higher-fidelity study is warranted only if the selective diagnostic clears at least 20% higher total P&L, the ₹2,500 annual floor, positive incremental P&L after its best five days, and positive incremental P&L under doubled execution friction. Keep admissions and shared risk fixed for this diagnostic. Compare November 5 using its exact observed futures marks if available. No orders, runtime, migration, settings or expiry change. Rollback is to retire the experiment.
