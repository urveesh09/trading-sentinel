# Round 32. A cash-reserved credit vertical at the same directional signal

Measure a distinct payoff on existing N11T clocks before investing in a two-leg replay. A long option can lose when the move is too small. A directional credit vertical can collect premium with capped gross risk once both legs fill. It also gives up upside and adds costs. Compare against the native parent as a fixed-admission diagnostic. If it earns a full lifecycle test, retain the trend-aware exit as a reference and give the vertical a genuine independent exit. [Vertical payoff mechanics](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/bull-put-spread-credit-put-spread).

On a fresh flat-book, one-lot N11T admission, use a bull put vertical for a long signal and a bear call vertical for a short signal. Sell the nearest strike at or below the observed future for puts and at or above it for calls, on the 50-point grid. Buy the 50-point farther out-of-the-money hedge in the same dated series. Keep native expiry exclusions. Require exact positive-volume minute prices on both legs and reported volume of at least 65 units. The entry additionally requires 5,000 OI and at most 1.5% modeled spread on each leg. Use the modeled bid for a sale and ask for a buy. The archived close and measured spread bands provide a price model. The tape supplies no order depth, priority or simultaneous packet.

Use the existing fee schedule with the correct buy/sell orientation on each leg. Apply the native ₹205 adverse allowance per leg and double it in stress. Reserve a ₹250 fee buffer, the complete strike width, the full hedge purchase, and both execution allowances in cash. Reserve against the saved entry-time pool and 15% cap. Admit only if credit exceeds that fee buffer and both allowances, and the theoretical gross maximum loss plus the same allowances and fee buffer is within the native parent's modeled premium backstop loss including its charges and allowance. The contractual cap depends on holding both legs. Closing early can incur additional crossing and fees.

Use the native parent’s saved close clock only for this pricing diagnostic. A missing closing print stays unresolved. Retain separately reported earned-child P&L, and avoid claiming the same child or the same subsequent account path would occur under a different parent. Preserve the original historical gates for an actual N012 candidate. Advance to a full shared-wallet test only with at least 20 priced changes, no unresolved entries, at least ₹10,000 combined improvement in the fixed-clock screen, no annual decline worse than ₹2,500, and positive increments after removing the five best days at both execution allowances. No runtime or expiry changes. November 5 requires action-clock-bound quotes for both legs and remains a separate observed check. Rollback is to retire the study.
