# Round 33. Cash and futures basis as an independent direction input

Test whether the cash index adds direction to an otherwise identical futures-only forecast. Research finds lead–lag relationships on minute horizons, with dependence on the market and study period. A futures lead alone gives little reason to expect a tradable prediction of the futures itself. [Khan et al. on NIFTY](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2022/2164974), [Kawaller et al. on intraday lead–lag](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/dynamics-of-stock-index-and-stock-index-futures-returns/6C0C02141F02160D48AB9CA1FDFB2785).

At 11:00 IST use the fifteen exact five-minute-spaced closing marks from 09:49 through 10:59 of the dated nearest NIFTY future and the cash index. Require positive futures volume and complete positive closes. Normalize basis as log future divided by cash. The two added inputs are the current basis minus the median through 10:54 and its last five-minute change. The reference uses a constant, the futures return since 09:49 and the last five-minute futures return. This construction removes the earlier session level and keeps the same contracts on both sides of every comparison. Require the dated regular options calendar and exclude expiry of either series.

Predict the signed front-future return from the exact 11:01 to 11:10 marks. Train both regressions on identical earlier dates, using the latest 252 complete sessions with at least 60. Score the feature using the original paired criteria of at least 200 predictions, 2% lower MSE, two percentage points higher sign accuracy and positive paired points after removing the best five incremental dates. A second gate uses an expected move of at least 25 points from the 10:59 decision mark and subtracts a fixed 15-point hurdle from each scored futures move. Require at least 20 strong predictions and positive total after that hurdle and the best-five removal. This is a feasibility screen before priced option or portfolio testing. No stop, forecast target, or cash or option fill is inferred from a future mark.
