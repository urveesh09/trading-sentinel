The native stand-aside joins a below-usual volatility reading to the last 20 independent shadow trades across setup families and sides. The partial 2022 diagnostic found that the gate sat out some profitable trades. Removing it indiscriminately is unsafe because it protected other windows. Test using the paper results of the candidate setup and side, measured before that session. The adaptive-markets hypothesis motivates a changing opportunity set. It does not establish which cohort works here. [Lo, author manuscript](https://web.mit.edu/Alo/www/Papers/JPM2004.html).

At each low-volatility native signal, aggregate matching one-lot shadow results by completed date. Use up to 20 distinct dates in the preceding 180 calendar days and require at least eight. Average multiple closes per day, cap each daily observation at ±₹2,500, and estimate its mean and one standard error. A mean minus the standard error above ₹200 gives positive evidence. A mean plus the standard error below −₹200 gives negative evidence. The interval is a decision heuristic. Today and future outcomes never enter it. Insufficient or ambiguous evidence preserves the existing decision.
