# Cross-index feasibility for regular F&O

A second exchange could use idle paper time even when the same-market cap is generous. The least correlated architecture is a **calendar sleeve** for ordinary SENSEX only on dates where NIFTY cannot enter under its own calendar. It avoids simultaneous directional inventory. A later separate arm could route idle NIFTY opportunities to SENSEX on ordinary dates if actual books support it. Start with data availability and exchange-specific spread evidence, without reading outcomes on possible holdout dates.

This inventory reports SENSEX minute candles by kind and month from a read-only connection. It lists simultaneous observations and potential calendar-sleeve days from dated series identity. No date is declared complete from row counts alone. Verify instrument coverage, dated holiday exceptions, cash and NIFTY borrowing of volume before scoring. Current BFO contracts, lot identity and exchange costs are separate from NFO. [Dated BSE expiry revision](https://www.indiainx.com/circulars/20250718-1/20250718-1.pdf), [dated NSE expiry revision](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP68747.pdf).

For spread calibration use the already inspected September–October 2026 paper archive. Pick every fourth session from the full ordered dates between September 10 and October 28. For each index read the nearest dated series at 09:45–15:10, exclude its expiry, deduplicate and require raw-verified provider age at most ten seconds. Require positive bid and ask and sufficient displayed size on both sides. Compare P50/P75/P90 and the fraction of packets above the regular 1.5% spread cap by premium bin. Displayed quotes establish the spread input. Actual queue, modeled one-minute clocks, fills and shared-wallet profits remain separate.

Do not add two independent P&L paths as a single capital ledger. After freezing code and testing a stand-alone BFO control, drive both through one replay account with common timestamps, NIFTY-first ordering, per-underlying reentry levels, chronological cash and exact exchange costs. Reconcile the small number of N11-era quote days before relying on archived counterfactual results. Keep 2025/2026 coverage limits and already-inspected windows explicit. No Breeze calls, broker calls or configuration changes.
