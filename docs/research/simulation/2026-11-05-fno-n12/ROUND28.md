# Round 28. Test whether early option activity adds direction

Prior option-wing research used prices. This test uses changes in listed open interest and unsigned turnover. Published option-volume research used transactions identified by who initiated an opening trade. Our minute data do not contain that field. The features represent aggregate activity only. [Pan and Poteshman](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10925), [Fodor, Krieger and Doran](https://ircommons.uwf.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Do-option-open-interest-changes-foreshadow-future/99380090633606600).

On regular NIFTY dates, at 10:00 use the front future’s 09:29 and 09:59 minute closes. Hold three strikes fixed for both snapshots, nearest 50-point strike at 09:29 and its immediate neighbors. Require both sides of the dated nearest non-expiring option series at every clock from 09:29 through 09:59. Require positive OI, price and traded volume in each endpoint for all six contracts. Intermediate volume can be zero. Use the change in log aggregate call-to-put OI from 09:29 to 09:59 and the log call-to-put volume over 09:30–09:59. Refuse missing values instead of moving the basket after the market moves.

Forecast the log return of the front future from 10:01 to 11:00. Require both positive-volume outcome marks. Fit a reference linear model with a constant and the 09:29–09:59 futures return. Fit the activity model with the same training observations plus the two chain features. Use the latest 252 eligible completed sessions and a minimum of 60. Sort and deduplicate the five archived panels. Each prediction uses only earlier sessions. Compare paired MSE, directional accuracy, signed futures points after the five best incremental days, and a fixed 15-point cost hurdle on model predictions of at least 25 absolute points. The hurdle is a coarse requirement before option pricing.

Advance only with at least 200 paired predictions, 2% lower MSE, a two-percentage-point sign accuracy improvement, and positive paired incremental points after dropping the best five days. The strong-prediction sample must also have at least 20 signals and positive points after the cost hurdle and its five best days. Compare November 5 separately if the exact six-chain observations and future marks are available. Nothing here changes a paper or broker trade. Rollback is to retire the experiment.
