# Round 24. Combine the mechanisms that improved something

Round 2's recovery override was profitable in aggregate and depended on a few days. Round 3's chain fallback improved one trade with no measured change in the other four windows. Round 5's early invalidation saved money in two windows and cut a much larger winner in 2026. Round 18's prior India VIX input improved the estimate of market movement, while the later use of that forecast to change profitable trailing stops lost money.

`ENTRY_MIX` uses the original Round 2 recovery override and Round 3 chain fallback together inside the native N11T account. A recovery opportunity must still pass the original breakout and persistence checks at one lot. An alternate strike is considered only on the original eligible planning refusals. The shared account must admit and fund the chosen order, and a fallback cannot expand the size of a recovery order. This arm tests interactions through one sequence of admissions, reservations, exits and risk decisions.

`CONDITIONED_MIX` adds the original Round 5 early invalidation only on sessions where the frozen prior India VIX model forecasts at least 10% less volatility than the realized-only forecast. Compare predicted standard deviations, which are the exponent of half the difference of the two log-variance forecasts. Missing forecasts leave native exits in force. The 10% threshold is a predeclared material difference. It tests whether already-weak positions are worth less recovery time when an independent input predicts smaller movement. It supplies no direction and does not change the native stop, trail or profit protection. Compare this arm both to the entry mix and to N11T.

Both arms use the regular NIFTY single-leg lifecycle with separate ₹250,000 starting wallets per development window. Freeze the code, prior rule implementations, input forecasts and configurations before running. Require exact N11 and N11T controls and the original aggregate, downside, annual and concentration gates before doubled execution stress or forward validation. Record how often each component changes the book and examine downstream cash and risk effects. November 5 requires eligible inputs observed before the decision and exact option-chain evidence for any fallback. Scope is Dev research. Rollback is to retire the experiment. No runtime files, migration, settings or order authority change.
