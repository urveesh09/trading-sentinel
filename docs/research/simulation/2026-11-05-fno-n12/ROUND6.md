# Round 6. Is usable paper capacity actually scarce?

The capital audit found premium in use during about 3–8% of the regular market clock, with peak open premium roughly 8–18% of starting equity. Many idle observations had no native signal. Premium-cap refusals occurred in 2025 and 2026. A larger cap can only improve the subset where a credible trade already exists and current capital, rather than the signal or risk budget, is the constraint.

- CAP20 is a diagnostic comparing a 20% aggregate premium cap with the native 15%. Preserve the per-trade budgets, halt thresholds, lot limits, concurrent position limits, calendar, all signals, all stops and execution costs. This tests ordinary use of the higher cap in both new positions and earned lots.
- CAP_LOCKED allows 20% for entry evaluations only while exactly one older position is open, with no open child. It must have an active native trail, signed trail more than 0.5 initial R in profit, a fresh sized bid, and a modeled stop outcome that covers the parent's entire entry plus all costs. The original 15% applies outside those conditions and on add-ons. Allow no other change.

Do not select on increased exposure. Compare cash used and incremental net, worst day and closed drawdown. Both arms use ₹250,000 and are separate from the ₹15,000 diagnostic. All dates are seen development evidence. No source/runtime or broker changes.
