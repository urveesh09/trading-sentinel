# Round 27. A continuation funded by booked profit

Earlier earned lots added exposure while the parent was open. Raising its stop sometimes cut the profitable parent. This test leaves every native exit and native add rule in place. It gives a new one-lot position a chance only after a successful native position has closed and the book is flat. Gains must already be settled in the shared ₹250,000 paper wallet. Research about conditional momentum motivates treating continuation and sharp reversals separately. The published horizons and instruments differ from these trades. [Daniel and Moskowitz](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439).

`BANKED_RETEST` waits for a fresh native settlement with positive net, a profitable underlying exit at least 0.75 original R beyond entry and at least ₹1,800 in total realized same-day net. Evaluate from 10:15 to 14:00. Use the first eligible setup within 30 minutes of settlement. Native signals retain priority. The two most recent completed five-minute candles must both start strictly after the native exit. The older close must be 0.15–0.60 original R back from the exit mark. The newer candle must close beyond that exit mark in the original direction, with a directional body of at least half its range. The current future must remain beyond the exit mark by at most 0.35 original R. Place the stop 0.10 original R beyond the adverse extreme of those two candles, with actual structural risk of 0.25–1.25 original R. Target 2.25 times structural risk. At most one qualified planning attempt per session.

Use the native N11T planner, entry window, own-expiry exclusion, stand-aside, risk halts, normal cash reserve, quote gates and daily trade cap. Cap the add at one lot with no earned children, and use the existing TPB exit lifecycle. Model the premium backstop including fees and stress within the lesser of ₹2,000 and half the day's booked gain. A stop delay or gap can exceed that planned loss. Do not make the stop smaller than one tick or admit when it lies at or above the entry bid. Preserve all other positions, and account for chronological effects on later native entries.

Freeze the candidate and five archived datasets before scoring. Require exact N11 and N11T parity, report the new tickets separately and apply the original net, worst day, annual drawdown, annual floor, concentration and completeness criteria. Run doubled cost and untouched evidence only after a development pass. For November 5 check actual settled cash and whether the book was flat during the candidate window. This experiment is Dev research only. No runtime, configuration, migration, broker authority, expiry or deployment change. Rollback is to retire the arm.
