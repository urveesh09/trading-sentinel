# Round 4 freeze

The overlap guard helped 2022, 2023 and 2024, then cost about ₹8,950 in 2025. Blanket filters risk eliminating genuine second entries. Shift the objective from eliminating existing trades to finding uncorrelated times to use idle capacity. Earlier broad intraday routers, gap fades and late momentum failed outside their development conditions. Test two distinct structures with full economics.

OPEN_FLIP seeks a failed overnight displacement by 11:00. Require an opening gap at least 25% of the prior whole session range and outside the prior final hour's range. The last two completed five-minute closes must be back through the relevant boundary after the preceding close was outside, with the session VWAP on the trade side. Use the prior close as target, a recent three-bar stop, 0.5–1.5 ATR initial risk and at least 1.8R of potential. Every check uses only the current or prior completed bars.

MIDDAY_COIL seeks a delayed continuation between 11:00 and 13:50. Opening 30-minute net displacement must exceed 60% of its range. The next-to-last four closed bars form a coil no wider than 45% of the opening range, preserving at least 65% of opening displacement. A completed break must be within 35% of that opening range beyond the coil edge. Use the coil's opposite boundary for a 0.5–1.5 ATR risk band and a 2R target.

The native signal has priority. A new arm may admit one trade per day only when the book is flat and the normal stand-aside allows it. One lot. Shared cash, loss brakes, costs, re-entry check, DTE and native plan gates apply. The native TPB exit wrapper is reused and the account must incorporate every downstream opportunity change. These are seen-year tests.
