# Round 16. Long-volatility opening compression

Test a cash-funded NIFTY straddle with its own timing in the ₹250,000 N11T account. The condor could not earn its four-leg costs and was missing a majority of usable chains. This test pays two premiums and seeks a renewed move after a wide opening hour followed by compression. A long straddle payoff motivates the mechanism. The study makes no claim of observed implied-volatility mispricing. [CME option payoff guide](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/files/2022/25-proven-strategies.pdf).

Let native entry run first. From 11:00 through 11:03, consider only the first available clock on a flat day with no prior entry, native halt or recorded crisis regime. Exclude the expiry date and require one or two calendar days to the dated front option expiry. Require all 21 completed five-minute bars from 09:15 through 10:55. The 10:15–11:00 range must be at most half the opening hour's range, and the last completed futures close must be in the middle half of that opening range. Buy one 65-unit lot each of the nearest-ATM call and put only when the combined ask is at most the opening hour's width in index points. Fix the strikes at the decision clock and use both on the following clocks without substituting contracts.

Buy the cheaper leg at the following minute close, then the other leg at the next minute close if the combined paid and current ask still meet the same limit. Abort and sell the first leg if the second is unavailable or no longer fits. Both legs need sized modeled top-of-book plus a strictly positive actual option candle volume at every used quote clock. Reserve the entire paid debit, a round-trip fee bound and ₹400 at entry with a ₹9,000 ceiling. Count an opened first leg toward the daily entry cap and block additional positions until the ticket has settled. Take both bid quotes on the observation after the combined mark reaches +₹3,000 net, −₹1,800 net, or 14:45. A missing bid delays settlement until the hard-flat deadline, then it is unresolved and the affected continuation is withheld. Single-leg abandonment includes ₹200 extra plus broker costs. Mark-to-execution gaps can exceed the planned stop loss.

Run all five development years against the exact frozen N11 and N11T receipts and original downside/materiality bars. Include staged fills, opportunity cost, chain refusals and unresolved cases. Doubled friction follows only a base pass. Quote depth and fills remain modeled on real historical prices. No new provider request, scheduler, broker or runtime change. Roll back by retiring this isolated research arm.
