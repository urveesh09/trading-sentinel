# Round 5. Structure-aware exits

The control made ₹53,667 from eight 2024 trail exits, ₹71,424 from nine in 2025 and ₹61,296 from six in 2026. Reducing losers and retaining the large right tail are separate jobs. Earlier exit ablations found a generic stagnation exit unsafe across members. Testing narrower and wider fixed exits alone repeats those results. Kaminski and Lo show stopping rules depend on return dynamics, which motivates the split below. [Author manuscript](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/bb69ca4b-0cdc-487f-831d-63b2e84fafee).

- INVALIDATED is an early loss exit. Between 10 and 35 minutes after entry, require best progress below 0.5 initial R, two fully completed five-minute closes below the trigger candle's adverse edge by at least 0.1 R, a live future below the same edge, and a fresh bid below entry. For puts, reverse all underlying inequalities. Require the threshold to sit inside the initial risk interval. Leave all native exits ahead of it.
- RUNNER is a bounded waiver for a post-target native trail exit, before 14:45. Both recent fully closed candles must overlap the trend. Their closes must have moved in the trade direction from the preceding close, and current futures must stay on the favorable side of the adverse edge of the two bars plus a 0.15 ATR buffer. Current progress must stay above 0.75 initial R and the fresh bid above entry plus twice the full modeled cost. Preserve the native trail ratchet and all other reasons, including any premium/profit lock, hard flat and initial stop. No additional lots.

Run both through the frozen yearly book, including management-induced changes to later entry opportunity. Record triggers and changes. These are development comparisons across the same five NIFTY windows. A large winner alone cannot pass. No runtime or production files are part of the experiment.
