Earlier studies forbid reuse of proceeds and allow at most two original tickets.
The earned-ticket study grew reserve/size from cumulative book profits; the
kicker studies bought another farther option. Neither tests repeated **same
original entry** tickets subject to a funded daily loss ledger. This round does.

Keep E03.1 entry, original nearest eligible contract, 1,250 ticket ceiling,
three-lot cap, management, five-minute cooldown and single open position.
Same own 25,000 cash and absolute 1,500/3,000/4,000 drawdown brakes. Baseline
control must reproduce every recorded day/position/event before scoring.

Two primary candidates, frozen before score:

1. **NET_RISK_LEDGER**: remove the gross-premium/day and two-entry count limits.
   Admit another original signal only when its complete worst-case purchase
   reserve fits `daily_loss_allowance + realized_day_net`, cash and 1,250.
   The daily loss allowance starts at the old 2,500 and scales by the same
   drawdown brakes. Returned principal may turn over; unclosed marks/trails
   never fund entry. After reserving a new ticket, realized net minus full
   ticket loss must remain >= minus that allowance, including exit-order fees.
2. **PROFIT_REENTRY**: first two tickets follow the original gross budget.
   After those close, another signal may use **only positive realized daily
   net**, inside cash/1,250 limits. Giving back that day's profits is possible;
   no additional debit may cross below day-opening cash. No bigger contract,
   extra wing or future best-time selection. Finite session and original
   five-minute cooldown bound entry count; no new entry-count parameter.

For new entries choose the original affordable 1,250 contract first, then resize
the same contract down to the actual risk room. Missing/unaffordable means no
entry, not a new farther-strike universe. Original first-two behavior of
PROFIT_REENTRY uses the original remaining gross budget exactly. Later cash
and brakes can diverge between counterfactual books, so do not force parity of
subsequent days. Full-price reserve protects a long ticket's loss; a stop price
does not. Fees/cash identities are independently checked.

If any position lacks executable closure, retain full-premium loss bound,
unresolved count and stop the simulated book; following dates are unavailable,
