# Primary-source findings, November 3, 2026

No web article supplies an untested trading rule. These sources explain which
information a hypothesis requires, and why prediction can still lose after costs.

- [Kanniainen & Magris: Option market (in)efficiency after return jumps](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12200).
  In S&P 500 minute quotes, some implied-volatility adjustments after jumps are
  delayed/asymmetric. Their reported statistical pattern does not establish
  abnormal returns after actual spreads. This supports testing both anticipatory
  price changes and economic execution, rather than assuming observed drift pays.
- [Michael, Cucuringu & Howison: Option Volume Imbalance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09319).
  Their forecasting uses volume decomposed by participant classes and concerns
  overnight equity returns. Our aggregate one-minute volume/OI has neither that
  identity information nor that forecast horizon. Its findings motivate a
  participation hypothesis, not a ready-made NIFTY expiry signal or signed flow.
- [Kapetanios et al.: Jumps in option prices and their determinants](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1386418118300168).
  Their high-frequency E-mini/S&P option study reports asynchronous jumps across
  contracts and the underlying. It motivates time-aligned cross-market evidence;
  a minute chart alone cannot establish who led or whether an arbitrage fill was
  available. U.S. microstructure findings are not an Indian strategy validation.
- [SEBI's January 16, 2026 CAS circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2026/introduction-of-closing-auction-session-cas-in-the-equity-cash-segment-and-certain-modifications-in-the-pre-open-auction-session_99122.html)
  and [NSE operational SOP](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR73362.pdf)
  establish the changed cash-close framework. The other study's auction data
  must remain separate from these 2024/2025 minute labels. No provisional index
  or old averaging formula is substituted for an official 2026 settlement here.
- [SEBI's current auction-review listing](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListingAll=yes&search=REVIEW)
  shows a further October 15 review. No later timing/settlement rule is silently
  back-applied to historical data; this study makes no new auction-trading claim.

Search and source scope: official exchange/regulator sources and original
research authors/publishers. No trader advice, profit screenshot or large
single-day account story is treated as verified net performance or a causal rule.
No online query accessed our broker, downloaded candles or disclosed credentials.
