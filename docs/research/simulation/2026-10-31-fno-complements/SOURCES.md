# Mechanisms and limits

- [Fidelity: DMI](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/DMI)
  and [ADX](https://www.fidelity.com/viewpoints/active-investor/average-directional-index-adx):
  trend strength and direction are separate readings. These explain indicators,
  not a profitable NIFTY-option rule. Our entry rule is a research hypothesis.
- [Dai, Medhat, Novy-Marx and Rizova, NBER 30917](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30917):
  liquidity and volatility affect the speed/persistence of short-run reversal.
- [Nagel, NBER 17653](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653): reversal returns vary
  with liquidity conditions. Neither paper validates our five-minute recovery
  rule or option execution; applying them here is explicitly an inference.
- [OCC/OIC: Theta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/theta):
  option time value decays. A useful index signal need not cover option costs.

Reviewed 2026-10-31. No vendor backtest was used as profitability evidence.
Prior Sentinel studies already rejected simple retests, failed breaks,
squeezes, gap fades, CPR and last-hour momentum. Repeating their parameters or
choosing only the profitable direction would create more selection bias.
