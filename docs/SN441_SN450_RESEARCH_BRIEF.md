## What earned another test

- **Timing is still the largest single hindsight advantage on this panel.** The original means use 105 priced BASE decisions and 117 priced TIMING decisions. On the 105 common cells across the six arms, timing still ranks first among single decisions and produces +₹2,194 / +₹1,996 at ₹205 / ₹400. It chooses from as many as 61 minutes, including minutes before the base signal, changes the 1-ITM contract as the market moves, and shifts the exit clock. Its rank therefore motivates experiments on waiting and price selection. The report cannot identify a live timing rule from it. The source also returns the last available mark *before* a missing scheduled exit. A synthetic example priced a 60-minute hold using its minute-five bid. Use an actual later fill or report unresolved in a companion. [SN428 code](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/sn428.py) · [SN428 receipt](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/results-sn428.json)
- **Gold-empty days have a higher directional hurdle than the headline.** For each clock and date, I reconstructed the original 1-ITM call and put and required both to be priced at both stresses. This matched 102 cells on 40 dates, with 36 cells on 14 Gold-empty dates. The average of the two sides is the expected coin value. The equation for an illustrative constant probability `p` of picking the better-paying side is `p × mean_better + (1 − p) × mean_worse = 0`. The implied threshold is about **58% / 67% overall** and **62% / 78% on Gold-empty days**. On those 36 cells the better/worse payoffs are +₹1,112 / −₹1,824 at ₹205 and +₹580 / −₹2,019 at ₹400. These thresholds are descriptive of this sample, payoff mix and exit. A live signal that selects different opportunities or gets high-payoff trades right at a different rate can have a different hurdle. [Pricing method](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/sn428.py)
- **Five seconds is an execution lead.** On the 252 one-morning opportunities the 5-minute gross result is +₹15.2 per original decision for CONFIRM-5 and +₹14.9 for the random wait. Applying the existing fee function and ₹205 stress to the priced rows gives about −₹64 for CONFIRM-5 versus −₹194 for IMMEDIATE per original decision. This mechanical comparison reuses the original fills and zeroes rows without a priced outcome, including unresolved ones, so it serves only as a gross-to-stress illustration. The scorer's `held()` accepts one valid packet at 0.9 seconds as five seconds of persistence even if the rest of the window is unobserved. And the random delay was 0–30 seconds, so it cannot isolate a five-second wait. [SN432 code](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/sn432.py) · [SN432 receipt](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/results-sn432.json)
- **₹205 is a reasonable replay reference.** The 41 paired rounds came from the general `FNO_PAPER` source over 28 dates. All 41 matched rows have a null strategy field. This sample preceded Gold's paper launch, compares top-of-book paper prices, and uses a minute close that can be later than the paper fill inside the same minute. The median ₹6.5 and mean ₹59 are paper/model differences from this proxy. Keep ₹400 as a tail test and qualify actual Gold and challenger costs separately. Treat the ₹617 as historical Gold replay per scheduled market session at the ₹205 assumption. [SN440 source](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/sn440.py) · [Broker fee schedule](https://www.zerodha.com/charges)
- **The exit, feed and expiry results tell us where effort is unlikely to pay.** The checkpoint and lock studies repeatedly lost more from clipped winners than they saved. Three seconds-data expiry candidates and two common quote cases are very little evidence, and seven morning signals a year give limited coverage. A true risk limit at ₹30k needs a viable contract under the loss room. SN434 tests Gold's normal strike first and then cuts the lot count, leaving a gap in the comparison of affordable alternative strikes. ## The ten slots

### SN441 — Build a common decision ledger for direction

Use two-sided options priced on the same decision set, with exact receipt times where available and a dated contract selected before the decision. Use both possible sides at each clock to get the exact coin expectation. Retain unaffordable, missing quote and unresolved rows. At the scheduled exit use the next observable legal bid after arrival or record unresolved. Keep expiry-day cutoffs fixed across arms. Publish the two payoff averages, which side wins, per-market-session net, and block uncertainty by date. Run the original comparison for parity and use common dated opportunities for the main diagnostic. The synthetic late-exit failure is a test fixture. **Learning** is how much direction a strategy needs on Gold-empty days and how much of the estimate was caused by cost, affordability or quote alignment. [Primary research on data snooping](https://www.ssc.wisc.edu/~bhansen/718/White2000.pdf)

### SN442 — Separate the reward for a cheaper entry from waiting for a new move

At the original clock lock side, strike, expiry and max debit. Compare immediate execution with a fixed wait and the hindsight best price in the next 30 minutes only, all at a common notional exit deadline. Check a common full holding horizon as a diagnostic. Match by date and clock and count no-fills. Use the actual quote panel before a seconds trial. Compare with the original 61-minute window to show how much of SN428 came from reaching backward, changing strike and shifting the exit. **Learning** is the amount of timing headroom still available once a signal actually exists.

### SN443 — Wait for a better option price while the directional premise holds

Use one existing directional signal stream with a known decision time, initially on development only. Freeze its side, strike and underlying invalidation point. During a short fixed window, allow one capped buy if the walked ask improves by a prespecified rupee amount beyond the same-direction premium change explained by the latest underlying price. Estimate delta from contemporaneous neighboring contracts with a predefined calculation. Demand fresh two-sided quotes, and cancel if the underlying premise fails or the interval ends. Compare with immediate arrival, equal-delay entry, and a plain discount-only limit. Begin with the same exit on each arm. This isolates whether waiting for an option-specific discount works without selecting mornings when the initial direction is already failing. **Learning** is whether observable option repricing provides a cheaper way into an existing opportunity, measured after fees and missed trades. If the paired study shows no advantage, close the branch. [Primary work on option price impact and spreads](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2021.100675)

### SN444 — Freeze an honest five-second execution companion

Fix the contract, direction, cap and original exit rule. Compare immediate legal capped arrival, unconditional five-second delay, and CONFIRM-5. Use per-level cap enforcement from `fw7c`, and fix a maximum allowed gap between valid quotes, including start and end. The synthetic one-packet window must fail. Charge all fills including commissions and apply the stops and the unresolved exit rule. Report paired fill improvements plus opportunity cost from misses per original scheduled decision. Run on already specified incoming signals with a future freeze. One minute sampled bars can be useful background. The test requires recorder depth. **Learning** is whether persistence improves the fill after controlling for the delay itself. [Kite packet specification](https://www.kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/websocket/) · [Primary research on latency and liquidity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04116)

### SN445 — Verify costs at the actual clock and order size

Repeat SN440 against the same known event on a synchronized quote source. Use only complete bars for the model reference and an explicitly measured quote-to-arrival displacement. Track the full-lot ask-to-bid walk, fees, market movement between timestamps and missed exits separately. Book costs per order using the dated fees and actual contract lots. Use the Gold shadow and recorder evidence when available to compare one-lot versus scaled Gold at its actual entry and exit times. Preserve insufficient rows. **Learning** is where ₹205 fits and whether the larger lots or opening candidate need a separate tail allowance. [SN440 source](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn426/sn440.py) · [Zerodha's dated STT change](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated)

### SN446 — See whether a different strike can meet the full-loss bound

Before rejecting the original signal, try at most the normal Gold strike, ATM and one step OTM on the same expiry and side. Select the highest measured delta within that defined set that fits the remaining day room at the walked premium plus entry and potential closure fees. Keep a common no-add lot and the original signal expiration, exit and expiry policy. Use the ₹6k initial loss room for a ₹30k start and verified dated lots. Compare with SN434, and report which available choices are weaker because of delta, decay or illiquidity. **Learning** is whether the signal can still pay when the whole debit is affordable to lose. Keep this separate from the ordinary stop-based book. Include the remaining loss room on all same-day signals and count unresolved exposure.

### SN447 — Use the supplied timestamps to run the bank-news screen

Use the [15-row filing handoff](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn441/nse-bank-filings-pilot.csv). It transcribes the earlier public NSE pilot for HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, SBIN, AXISBANK and KOTAKBANK, January–July 2026. `exchange_disseminated_at_ist` is `exchdisstime` and `exchange_sequence_id` is `seq_id`. The query used NSE's `/api/corporate-announcements` with `index=equities`, the issuer symbol and Jan–Mar or Apr–Jul date windows. The screen included results-titled filings and board outcomes whose attachment description mentioned financial results. Treat them as candidate metadata. Reconcile first release, revisions and duplicate issuer-dates. For example, Kotak has two timestamps on July 18 and several banks share that date, so count overlap by reaction session. Pair next-session abnormal 15-minute returns with ordinary days having similar opening gaps. The early screen can start from the existing stock bars. A trade test still needs affordable option quotes, verified chronology and adequate independent dates. **Learning** is whether the release adds information beyond the market's first reaction. [Official NSE filings](https://www.nseindia.in/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements) · [Primary earnings price discovery study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1475-679X.12394)

### SN448 — Test a repeatable information clock in the middle of the Indian day

Use the LSE main market's 08:00 London cash open, which falls at **12:30 or 13:30 IST** depending on UK summer time. Fix the clock mapping from official holidays and `Europe/London`. On existing NIFTY data, compare a short prespecified post-open window against the other Indian clock on the same day, and test both sides of the summer-time changes. Treat the periods with the London clock closed or the alternate IST clock as controls. Check whether residual movement after the first completed local bar is large enough to price. Only with an eligible timestamped common-session UK futures or opening-basket series test whether its signed surprise makes an incremental NIFTY forecast. Avoid assigning direction from the calendar itself. **Learning** is whether the useful trading window moves with an outside information event or merely reflects an Indian time-of-day pattern. The usual early exit restriction still governs expiry mornings. [LSE main market hours](https://www.londonstockexchange.com/en/press-releases/2026/london-stock-exchange-launch-lse-24) · [LSE market holidays](https://www.londonstockexchange.com/trade/trading-access/business-days) · [UK clock changes](https://www.gov.uk/when-do-the-clocks-change)

### SN449 — Estimate the achievable frequency and how long a verdict needs

For surviving research arms compute three numbers from earlier dates only. Measure the share of empty sessions with a genuine signal, the share it can afford and execute, and the net distribution on those opportunities. Compare the implied all-day gain with the ₹154 / ₹498 per empty-day target at the two stresses. With this support estimate a prospective sample size using date-block intervals or block simulation and a fixed minimum economic effect. Show how many trades occur in one date, which trades share a catalyst, and the effect of an affordable BANKNIFTY month-end cluster. Publish a plausible stopping condition and an evidence-insufficient branch when support is low. **Learning** is whether the route could cover the wage gap or can only provide occasional contribution, and whether a small forward sample would ever distinguish the two.
