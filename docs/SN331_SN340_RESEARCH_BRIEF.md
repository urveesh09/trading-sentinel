## What the last round changes

1. **The strongest gap-day evidence concerns which moves fail.** Buying a short break ranked below all 200 random-clock books in each 2019 quarter. The January–April seconds test lost ₹337 per break trade, while waiting for the pullback lost ₹357. Entering less often reduced total losses. Both directions still have to overcome option repricing, friction and the execution clock. The 12–20% result is a hindsight measure for the original breakout direction, the sampled opening-ATM option, and specified entry and exit windows. A new strategy needs a separate reachability estimate for its own direction, strike and clock. [Prior results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn316/README.md) · [Seconds scorer](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn316/sn319_322.py)
2. **Gold's best next test is transfer, not another early veto.** SN324 avoided ₹15,670 of losses and missed ₹36,114 of winners at ₹205. None of the 86 losing trades broke N11’s setup level in the first minute. Finish the pre-existing independent N11/Gold replay before fitting a new Gold rule. WK-H passed its pre-registered crash exam as modeled, and the March 13 halt fell between its weeks. That is a good result for the separate margin-funded paper leg. Gold v8 is the v5 signal and risk policy carried into a different execution lifecycle. [SN323–SN328](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn316/README.md)
3. **The V8 intent logger has no position lifecycle.** A synthetic fixture with a ₹30k wallet, two paper lots of 65 units and a ₹235 ask produced a 65-unit V8 entry intent and a 130-unit exit intent. Runtime `sn_gold.py` passes the paper quantity to the latter. The records are useful evidence of requests. Fill-capable replay needs its own position, costs, pending quantity and actual fill clock. The existing `buy_fees` helper calls the round-trip fee function with a zero exit. That function still charges two brokerages. This conservatively reserves some exit charges. Distinguish actual cash paid, cash reserved and independently charged order costs in the next ledger. [V8 intent code](../python-engine/sn_gold_v8.py) · [Gold caller](../python-engine/sn_gold.py) · [Fee calculator](../python-engine/fno_costs.py)
4. **The refill denominator changes the answer.** SN327 measured a max calendar-month ratio of 62.8% or 80.9% under ₹205 or ₹400. A rolling 20-session window reached **95.7% or 122.8%**, respectively, measured against the wallet specified in that study. One spare wallet covered the first historical rolling stress and would have fallen short at the second. That merits a cash-flow replay of actual deposits, reserves and withdrawals. [SN327 receipt](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn316/results-sn327.json)
