## Ten slots and their outputs

### SN426 — Reconcile the dates and every entry decision

Build one row per exchange session from the five manifest-matched caches and dated contract masters. Resolve the 12 unmatched dates individually. Label regular, special, confirmed expiry, eligible with data loss, and unresolved separately. Then join raw N11 candidates, N11 pre-Gold refusals, Gold entries and Gold skips by bar, book, side and token. Keep duplicate R0.85/N11 events, busy-wallet rejects and new sessions with missing source evidence distinct. Report both per-market-session net and replay coverage. Confirm train/test boundary and no-P&L treatment of the absent dates. **Learning:** how many sessions and signals the deployed policy skips by design, and how many the pipeline failed to measure. [NSE dated specifications](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-specifications) · [SN422 source](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn411/sn422.py)

### SN427 — Test one Gold signal on expiry mornings with two contract choices

First reproduce the disabled-entry path. In an offline research arm, generate ordinary N11/R0.85 decisions before the shared expiry gate. Keep every other signal gate, then admit one long NIFTY lot in the morning and flatten by 13:30 IST. Compare the expiring and next-week option on the **same** decisions, with the same intended stop, one position, and fixed affordable strike rules. On common cases also compare similar entry delta where recorded. Use non-expiry Gold as a sanity check and expiry mornings without signals as the matched timing control. The existing archive inventory has 87 NIFTY dated 1-second receipts with corresponding 10-second bars from June 2023 to September 2026, mostly opening-ATM coverage. These can screen a mechanism. A comparison of two quoted tenors and a fill verdict need actual paired depth. **Learning:** whether the expiry gap comes from scarce ordinary signals, poor same-day option execution or a useful signal hidden behind a coarse day-level gate. [Expiry testing method](SN_TESTING_PROCEDURE.md) · [NSE expiry circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP68685.pdf)

### SN428 — Find which decision creates the hindsight profit

On one shared dated panel freeze the clock, the affordable contract rule, and a simple fixed hold/stop. Recompute a paired matrix that grants hindsight over just one decision at a time. Vary side, strike/expiry, exit or entry time. Include one joint side-and-exit diagnostic to measure interaction, and seeded causal timing and side controls. Use receipt-aligned quotes where possible, count the first observable stop and exit persistence, and report the distribution of capture fractions and the share of original dates reaching ₹750. Mark expired or unpriced cells explicitly. This shows whether better direction would cover costs with a fixed exit, how much exit timing is worth with an ordinary entry, and which part has little headroom. Hindsight outputs stay outside strategy P&L. [SN412 source](research/fno/2026-11-17-sn411/sn412.py)

### SN429 — Test whether the trade still deserves its remaining time

Keep the original entries for one research stream with enough matched fills, such as the general opener as a diagnostic. At a single registered checkpoint, compare exiting at the next executable bid with holding to that entry's existing exit. Estimate that paired difference on older dates using only elapsed time, underlying progress from entry and the contemporaneous net bid. Test one simple checkpoint policy on a fresh block. Keep the right-tail give-up and losses saved visible, including trades with missing checkpoint quotes. The same entries and fees apply to both arms. **Learning:** whether continuation after the checkpoint has value that can be detected before exit. Stop the exit-policy branch if it just removes the large winners, as earlier fast-target studies did. [Earlier results on profit caps](SN_PROBLEM_STATEMENT_EMPTY_DAYS.md) · [Selective prediction and abstention](https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/el-yaniv10a.html)

### SN430 — Test delayed repricing after an actual public filing

Take the NSE dissemination timestamps and deduplicate by first release containing the results. Freeze one bank roster and one release family. For a weekend or after-close release, measure how much market- and peer-adjusted move remains after the next morning's first two completed 15-minute bars. Compare with the same stock or banking proxy on comparable sessions having the same opening gap and breadth without a filing. For the initial screen use available 15-minute constituent data and NIFTY, and match dates with cash and derivatives clocks. Rupee P&L requires a recorded one-lot option at a predefined expiry and side. Test a BANKNIFTY option only if the sector shows a basis for forecasting its own remaining move. This shows whether public company information contributes anything beyond what price already says. Stop before an option trial if release dates are too scarce or the move is spent by the causal entry time. Paper-compare the existing policy with a full-cash-loss version that admits a new long option only when the full premium and applicable charges fit the remaining day room. Quantify which strikes and signals disappear and what normal and no-bid cases would cost.
