# SN381–SN390 research brief

November 16, 2026 · Review of the SN366–SN380 Dev report and its source

## Decision

SN380 has enough evidence to finish a paper test. It has no basis for a live allocation or a daily payout. The narrower idea worth testing is that an opening-auction forecast may pay through an affordable in-the-money (ITM) option during the first hour. The next question is how much of the forecast is still available at an executable ask, after the option's opening premium, fees and the cost of exiting.

### Evidence behind the decision

- **The opening lead is modest.** SN376's 09:16 auction-direction arm averaged +₹67.30 per trade at ₹205 additional stress over 171 historical trades. Same-side ATM was −₹43.80 and the nominally matched ITM coin flip was −₹130.70. The directional ITM arm lost ₹136.50 per trade at ₹400. The older cash-index opening gap is a different signal, and its arm lost ₹169.60 per trade at ₹205. SN376 failed its frozen two-sample rule. SN380 is a prospective follow-up to one historical arm. See [round results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/README.md), [SN376 results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/results-sn376.json) and [frozen SN380](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/fw7.py).
- **Two scorer checks change what a positive result would mean.** In an isolated synthetic check, both strike selectors accepted a one-strike ITM fallback costing ₹19,500 against ₹15,000 of premium room. The forward scorer subsequently calls the entry helper without a fill-price cap. In a separate fixture, the forward judge returned PASS with 15 candidate fills when each control had only one shared fill. The candidate lost by ₹200 on that shared session. The judge compared the candidate's mean across all its fills with the control's mean on the shared subset. The forward loop also constructs its own qualifying date list after skips. Establish the actual common dates explicitly. See [historical selector](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/sn376.py), [forward selector](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/fw7.py), [forward judge](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn351/fw5.py) and [depth-based fills](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn301/fw2.py).
- **A forward signal can use a price received after its decision.** The historical opening loader used the 09:15 futures open as a fallback on 74 of 172 sessions in the preceding SN353b study. The forward loader accepts the first post-open futures packet as a fallback without bounding receipt by the 09:16 decision. A synthetic packet arriving at 09:16:20 set the 09:16 signal. The audit must record the source and receive time, and report the auction match and first continuous trade separately. The clean estimate requires all inputs to have been received by the original decision timestamp. See [historical source breakdown](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn351/results-sn353b.json) and [forward loader](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn351/fw5.py).
- **Signed flow is a clue about measurement.** SN379's 15 signals came from eight distinct dates. At 15 minutes and ₹205, the total falls from +₹2,727 to −₹2,917 once November 2 is removed. At 30 minutes it falls from +₹1,525 to −₹2,013 once October 5 is removed. Both holds were negative at ₹400 even with those days included. The repeat test needs packet-level trade signing and evidence across different days. See [SN379 event rows](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/results-sn379.json).
- **The Gold 2022 loss matters for funding.** The repaired replay reported −₹15,281 from ₹30,000 at ₹205, −₹23,511 at ₹400, and ₹53,896 of gross refill transfers at ₹205. The 2023 ₹30,000 start also lost money. These are observed adverse starts. Gross refill transfers can cycle the same cash and should be reconciled against the actual external reserve needed. The original 2022 source wiring omitted 21 months of daily data and was corrected with both outputs retained. The corrected run counted 190 sessions with option data against the exam sheet's target of 200, with a provision to score and disclose a smaller sample. The unfinished N10 continuous-account test is also relevant. See [2022 source correction](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/SOURCE_CORRECTION_2022.md), [N10 exam protocol](research/simulation/2026-11-01-fno-n10/EXAM_2022.md) and [round results](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn366/README.md).

## Ten slots

### SN381 — Check whether the winning observation could have been bought

Freeze a companion audit for SN380 before viewing forward P&L. Preserve its original output. For both ITM depths and the ATM control, compute lot size and the maximum buy limit from the available wallet cash, ₹15,000 floor and entry charges. Enforce the ceiling on the submitted order price and on verified whole-lot cost. Record signal and quote receipt times, cash before and after, and actual stop and exit timestamps. The coin-flip control should use the same depth and admissibility rules in the paired comparison. Include a one-lot decision ledger with distinct states for inadmissible, missed, filled and unresolved. Synthetic cases must cover the ₹19,500 fallback, a ₹15,000 top-of-book quote whose walk costs more, a stale quote, a fallback signal from a late packet, an unresolved exit and a one-matched-day control.

### SN382 — Replicate the opening lead on the exact same sessions

Publish the frozen 20-session verdict with the companion audit on a fixed recorder date list. Retain days without a signal or affordable fill in the per-session denominator. Compare P&L differences on genuinely common fills and separately compare both arms across all eligible sessions, with a genuine skip scored as zero and unknown outcomes disclosed. Inspect entry timestamp alignment and signal provenance before scoring. Use the first window to size a fresh validation block using observed variation by date or week and an economically relevant effect size. Require positive incremental P&L at ₹205 and ₹400, positive matched lift, and positive net after removing the best day before further consideration. Report uncertainty by date or week. Stratify historical diagnostics at the September 7, 2026 exchange opening-auction change and avoid pooling on the assumption the two auction protocols are equivalent. [NSE auction change FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-10/FAQs_on_Closing_Auction_Session_CAS_in_the_CM_and_changes_in_FO_Segment_ver3_20261023220345.pdf) · [Sullivan, Timmermann and White on multiple-rule testing](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00163)

### SN383 — Explain what the ITM option buys

On the same signal, decision time and expiry, log executable P&L for ATM, one ITM and two ITM with common admissibility, one lot and unchanged exit. Use quotes and the forward price to estimate how much of each leg's change came from underlying movement, implied-volatility repricing and time decay. Show bid–ask and charges separately, with a residual for model error. Compare returns per eligible day and show the opening time value paid as a fraction of the debit. The diagnostic should distinguish a cheaper way to express the forecast from a bigger directional bet with a higher premium. If they are both affordable and recorded, test one next-week contract with similar entry delta on common dates as a maturity check. Only promote a contract choice on later paper dates. [Muravyev and Ni on intraday option returns](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X19302193) · [Zerodha charges](https://zerodha.com/charges)

### SN384 — Cut a failed opening thesis while preserving the entry

Measure paired P&L at 09:36 and around 10:16 from the original 09:16 fills. Earlier results suggest the index forecast fades while the ITM option's historical 60-minute arm did better than its 20-minute arm. Compare a single prospective exit with the frozen 60-minute hold if opening economics warrant it. At 09:36, exit if the latest received front-futures price has given back at least half of the move from its previous comparable close to its auction match. Use the next eligible bid after latency. Keep the original ₹3,000 stop. Show after-cost improvement and the value of winners cut short. Reuse the same fills and show the cost stress on fixed executed exits as well as the existing simulation's stress-triggered exits, since added stress can change the stop time.

### SN385 — Test whether the futures opening move has broad cash support

Use exchange-published futures auction prices and component cash-auction matches received by 09:16. Weight the share of NIFTY constituents agreeing with the futures gap using weights available before that session. Compare it with the plain futures auction gap on the same sessions, conditional on gap direction and size. On dates when both futures maturities are auctioned and available, log whether their gaps from their respective prior close agree. This lets the agent check whether a seemingly strong front opening is broad-based or concentrated around roll. Start with a timestamp and constituent-weight manifest and a frozen coverage requirement. Score out-of-sample incremental forecast and affordable option P&L. NIFTY constituent 15-minute bars cannot reproduce a simultaneous auction. [NSE F&O pre-open mechanics](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-pre-open-session) · [Biais, Hillion and Spatt on pre-open price discovery](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/250095)

### SN386 — Test signed option pressure before the futures move

Use the recorded option trade/quote packets to check whether the volume changes can be tied to a trade against a contemporaneous bid or ask. Keep ambiguous volume unsigned. Use exchange time for sequencing when accurate and receive time for every trade decision. Freeze the first qualifying event per date and the 15-minute horizon. Compare with unsigned volume, matched same-clock and same-futures-move controls, and a time-shifted flow placebo. The option flow must improve the future's forecast after the current futures move is accounted for, then pay on the next tradable option quote. Report signable volume, date-level returns and gains without the best date. One-second and 10-second candle archives can check timing sensitivity and the size of available moves. The trade-sign test needs actual time-aligned trade and quote observations. [Jurkatis, Bank of England, on inferring trade direction](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2020/inferring-trade-directions-in-fast-markets)

### SN387 — Close the incomplete Gold exam

Reconcile the 242 market dates to the 190 option-usable dates before adding data. Account for exchange exclusions and entry gates separately from a missing strike, missing bar or warm-up gap. Audit the excluded dates by month, volatility and price path so the owner can see whether the exam omitted particular kinds of session. Where available, use 2019–2021 as chronological warm-up and for predeclared regime diagnostics. Check lot sizes, expiry/session calendars and fees applicable at the time. Reproduce the fixed N10 D comparison with a single 2022–26 account on its original ₹2.5 lakh terms, recording both original and any coverage-corrected outputs. The outcome should settle whether WATCH met its frozen condition. The ₹30,000 wallet needs its own comparison.

### SN388 — Find where Gold's existing risk filter helped or hurt

Trace exactly which paper exits were known when N11's stand-aside rule fired. Attribute the actual before-and-after P&L on suppressed signals to date, setup, contemporaneous market state and time required for the filter to recover. Compare the unmodified book, the frozen N10 WATCH comparison and its one-try versus retry rule on aligned data. Then replay a ₹30,000 Gold-sized book under common cash and execution constraints. Establish which position adds were ever affordable and report the contribution after incremental costs. The ₹560 N10 retry improvement in the ₹2.5 lakh 2022 exam is too small to infer that the ₹30,000 book improves. Decision criteria include better ₹400 results without worsening drawdown, refill funding or Gold capital availability on fresh paper dates.

### SN389 — Measure the cash needed to live through a bad start

Build the account ledger with opening cash, withdrawals, stop room, P&L, internal transfers and external deposits. Start with the existing ₹30,000 wallet and ₹30,000 reserve. Run chronological adverse starts at ₹205 and ₹400, including 2022–23 where coverage allows. Show maximum reserve depletion, gross transfers and actual external cash needed as separate figures. Report the share of market days taking home ₹750 and the longest dry spell, along with the average. SN374's model needed another ₹527 per Gold-empty session at ₹400. Use it as a comparison for the updated ledger and calculate each candidate's contribution after missed and unaffordable entries.

### SN390 — Make every survivor beat Gold through one wallet

Run one live-clock paper ledger against Gold-only on identical days. At 09:16 the system has no end-of-day Gold label. Admit a gap candidate only with an explicit Gold-priority rule at each event. Count candidate positions tying up cash when a Gold entry arrives, and use the actual candidate exit latency and bid before cash returns. Both books share a 20% day limit, with Gold rules applied as deployed and the candidate working with a ₹3,000 intended stop. Replay missing bids, overshoots, overlapping signals, fees and unresolved orders. Report net per market session, net on Gold-empty days, incremental net, blocked Gold entries, maximum reserve draw and payout distribution. A candidate that merely improves some empty days while worsening the one-wallet result fails.

Run SN381, SN382 and SN387 first. Run the mechanism work on recorded or explicitly designated development data. Freeze one opening variant only if the original opens a credible path at both stresses. SN385 or SN386 can earn a separate prospective paper test after its data and control checks. Use successive untouched windows for decisions and keep a register of every tried rule. These are research recommendations based on Dev results and synthetic reproductions of the wallet, scorer and timestamp issues. A new prospective result, live performance comparison or portfolio replay remains to be produced.
