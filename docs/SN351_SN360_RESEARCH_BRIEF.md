Research handoff at Dev `56ce16d3`, 16 November 2026. Each numbered study covers a mechanism, its controls and an economic verdict. No new strategy was scored. The existing SN300a and `fw3`/`fw4` outcomes remain unopened.

Gold v5/V8 remains the control. Start Gold at ₹30,000, keep Gold’s priority, its existing growth trigger and three-step cap, and the 20% shared day budget. Candidate instruments are fully paid long index options, one whole lot to start, with premium at most wallet minus ₹15,000 and the current ₹3,000 planned stop inside remaining day room. Keep existing trading deadlines and the early expiry cutoff. Futures, rates, currency and stocks can supply information without using the wallet. Exposure stays charged against cash while any exit is pending. Test after all costs at the agreed ₹205/₹400 stresses and report the difference at observed quote costs. ## Three consequential findings from the review

1. **The short-range family is exhausted on the tested histories.** The ₹95 disadvantage is relative to the selected random-clock books in SN344 and the related break study. It is an economic result for these entries, not a measured surcharge on every fast move or a universal verdict on bought options. SN331’s opposite side gained only ₹2–35 in the option mids against ₹90–105 of spread and charges. Moving thresholds or lowering the slippage estimate cannot fix that. The new predictive work needs an observation available *before* the bid/ask consumes the move. [SN331–SN350](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn331/README.md) · [SN331 receipt](research/fno/2026-11-16-sn331/results-sn331-345.json)
2. **The opening-auction data lead is grounded in a real capture.** A read-only, counts-only check of the completed Nov 16 Production recorder found 431 full NIFTY futures packets before 09:15. The nearer recorded future had **370 packets in 09:00–09:08**, all with nonzero aggregate buy/sell quantities, representing **269 distinct quantity pairs**. The farther future had only two packets in that window, with zero quantities. Those bytes establish that changing fields were received on the near future. Check their meaning, exchange timestamp and usable cutoff against published auction information before treating them as auction imbalance. Nov 16 remains a development session. NSE's futures auction began in Dec 2025 and publishes indicative prices and quantities. The test must use an ordinary post-open option quote for execution. [NSE futures pre-open rules](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-pre-open-session) · [Launch circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP69898.pdf) · [Kite packet spec](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/websocket/)
3. **The current wage estimate does not reconcile a self-financed growth transfer.** `lab.run` increases the sizing wallet and `pool_play` credits all realized daily P&L to the spare pool. Neither debits the extra cash allocated to the trading wallet. A synthetic fixture using those equations starts with ₹30,000 trading and ₹30,000 spare, books +₹30,000, and raises the wallet to ₹45,000. The old equations then have ₹60,000 in the spare pool and pay ₹15,000 after retaining ₹45,000. That accounts for ₹105,000 including the payout, against ₹90,000 actually available. If the growth is funded internally, the transfer leaves ₹45,000 trading and ₹45,000 spare, with no payout under the same reserve rule. The published ₹881/₹414 are outputs of the existing model. The corrected withdrawals, pauses and wage need a chronological replay. ## Ten studies

### SN351 — Locate where a new signal could pay

Build one shared opportunity panel using already-seen minute and one-second data for research and fresh packet data for executable checks. At fixed, non-overlapping decisions report the quoted cost of one lot, time until the first executable ₹300/₹750 gain, probability of being stopped first, and residual bid-to-ask payoff at fixed 5-, 20- and 60-minute horizons. Treat those horizons as diagnostics. Match on the underlying price move before entry, clock, expiry and premium. Add controls starting before a move, immediately after and after the spread normalizes. Report the hindsight ceilings and full event counts separately from realized returns. This tells the agent which information arrival and holding period have enough room to test, and whether the previous lack of opportunity was limited to entering around a spent move.

### SN352 — Reconcile the wage and refill ledger

Reproduce SN342 first, then replace the sizing-only transfer with a cash-funded journal. Every day reconcile starting equity plus realized P&L and owner deposits minus withdrawals to trading cash, reserved cash and any remaining position. Record margin as zero for this book. Trading wallet upgrades transfer cash from retained profit, with growth and reserve treated as distinct uses of money. Test, at the same finite initial funding and risk, retention for a monthly withdrawal and variable withdrawals capped by actual excess cash. Compare growth blocked by insufficient funding, days paused, amount paid, worst refill demand and the longest dry spell. A successful accounting test corrects the business result even if every new strategy fails.

### SN353 — Futures opening-auction imbalance and acceptance

Validate field semantics from official indicative snapshots and the recorded near-month future. Measure the sign, imbalance adjusted for total indicative size, and persistence across fixed 09:05 and 09:06 snapshots, before the random order-entry close. A price-only benchmark uses the indicative gap and previous close. A matched control uses the same opening gap, DTE and first-quote quality. Freeze one challenger that trades the first eligible post-open one-lot quote only if auction pressure persists into continuous trading, with one common price cap and a predeclared hold. Replace or abstain if the first executable quote has already repriced. Use the existing day cutoff and count all first tradable decisions, carries, misses and stale packets. Research initially compares price response for 5/20/60 minutes, then locks one horizon for the score. This is the lead candidate because it observes queued demand before the option trade rather than infer demand from a completed price burst. [NSE pre-open fields](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-pre-open-session)

### SN354 — Offshore information the local auction has yet to absorb

Use dated, timestamped GIFT NIFTY observations as a separate source. First require a licensed/authorized and reproducible source with actual time-of-day values. Official end-of-day reports alone cannot supply the decision clock. Align the same underlying and comparable expiry, previous-session basis, currency conventions and overlapping clocks. Test the offshore move that arrives *after* the domestic market closes, and then the residual versus the Indian indicative open. Compare plain GIFT direction, plain local gap, and one fixed overnight-residual rule with a fresh post-open entry. Match local gap and option spread so this is a test of what has yet to be priced, with convergence in the traded underlying and cost-adjusted option net. Use SN353 as a baseline and treat conflicting or delayed feeds as unavailable. Do not purchase or fetch inaccessible inputs as part of this proposal. [NSE IX reports](https://www.nseix.com/markets/reports) · [Indian intraday offshore/onshore study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1746880923000554)

### SN355 — Use a surprise and its transmission, not an event calendar alone

Create a point-in-time event ledger for one initially chosen family, preferably scheduled RBI decisions, with official release time, a genuinely pre-event expectation, released value and independent rate-market response. The economic question is whether a durable rates repricing leaves an option move *after* the first equity response and IV adjustment. Compare no-surprise events, events with disagreement across rates and equity, comparable non-event clocks and the prior calendar-only strategy. Separate a rate surprise from stronger economic news empirically rather than assuming that a hike always implies a put. If timestamped rate observations or enough independent releases are absent, this is an event feasibility study. Avoid extrapolating its limited frequency to a daily wage. [RBI surprise event study](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=22929) · [Jarociński and Karadi](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/mac.20180090)

### SN356 — Test the sector that was actually affected

Start with Financials as a prespecified sector. On timestamped heavyweight disclosures or a validated sector catalyst, decompose the bank basket’s move into a market beta component estimated only on earlier data and a sector residual. Require independent participation from multiple banks so one stock is not mistaken for a sector move. Compare broad-index-only, sector context, then the same event expressed through an actual affordable BANKNIFTY contract versus a NIFTY option if both are eligible. Prove native quote depth, lot cost and dated monthly expiry before scoring; catalog unpriced opportunities. Earlier NIFTY constituent and bank-proxy work asked whether the broad index moves next. This hypothesis asks if the sector itself has a payoff while the broad index stays flat. One cash-settled index option is the only trade. [NSE timestamped announcements](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements) · [NSE contracts](https://www.nseindia.in/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-specifications)

### SN357 — Test the move the option chain is already charging for

For a single candidate event from SN353, SN355 or SN356, compute what a held call or put would need to earn after its actual ask, expected exit spread, fee and stress. Use synchronized neighboring strikes and expiries to characterize the priced tails, with bid/ask bounds and a monotonic/convex sanity check. Treat chain-implied tails as risk-neutral maturity prices. Estimate the realized *intraday* path to break-even separately from observed outcomes in earlier blocks, with target-before-stop and missed fills. Compare premium only, ATM IV only and the incremental strike-based information at equal risk; avoid assuming a maturity probability gives a 20-minute probability. One long leg, chosen using information at the decision clock, is all the wallet trades. The goal is to learn whether a predictor's remaining opportunity is genuinely cheap across the chain, rather than just whether the ATM premium or IV looks low. [Breeden and Litzenberger](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/assets/documents/con_044170.pdf) · [Aït-Sahalia and Lo](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.215228)

### SN359 — Measure V8 return loss across a full order lifecycle

Finish the shadow in one package: position-level starts and cancels, observed quantity at all acceptable levels, queue/arrival assumptions, partial entry, scale-in, protective stop, failed exit, eventual close and recovery receipts. Compare fee-adjusted P&L of all original Gold decisions, including missed winners and positions without executable exits. Do not infer a full-size fill at the top quote just because aggregate five-level depth is enough. `fw4.v8_fill_realism` currently compares top price with aggregate quantity and skips intents with `limit is None`, so its output is a limited quote-touch diagnostic. Require a later depth-aware replay and realistic broker constraints before interpreting the shadow as money retained. Leave `fw4` frozen and report under its original definition. ### SN360 — Validate decision-time cash, genuine gap coverage and payout

Freeze at most two primary challengers for a new prospective cohort. Treat wider studies as mechanism/viability diagnostics until data and return headroom justify scoring. Chronologically replay Gold and each candidate with the conserved cash ledger, Gold-first ordering, pending cash, preemption and delayed exit. `fw4._arm` credits only sessions already labeled FLAT and tests debit/stop room; it does not model a candidate blocking a later Gold fill. Use its output as retrospective feasibility. Compare Gold alone against candidate plus Gold on all sessions. Report P&L on proven gap sessions, all-session incremental net, economic repeatability, per-earned-session frequency, opportunity costs, after-cost net with and without its best day, drawdown, refill needs and payable days. Require positive matched-control lift at both stresses and replication on unseen sessions. At one lot, a strategy filling a handful of days a month must still explain how much of the monthly wage it could fund.
