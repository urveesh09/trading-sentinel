### SN456 — Test new information from participant positioning

**Verified source.** NSE's public archive template is `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/nsccl/fao_participant_oi_DDMMYYYY.csv`. A dated probe returned eight valid files spanning 2023–2026. The date in each title matched the request, participant rows summed to `TOTAL`, and total index-futures longs equaled total shorts. Archive `Last-Modified` ranged from 18:55 to 20:12 IST on report dates. This corroborates end-of-day dating. Actual first availability belongs in a receipt. The only 404, November 20, 2024, was an official F&O holiday. The lot-size revision applied to newly introduced contracts from November 21, so old and new sizes could coexist. `Future Index Long` and `Future Index Short` aggregate contract counts across indices and expiries. Their ratios can move as composition changes. [NSE report catalogue](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/all-reports-derivatives) · [Example CSV](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/nsccl/fao_participant_oi_28102026.csv) · [Lot revision](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP64990.pdf) · [Holiday notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP64959.pdf)

**What to implement.** Parse `Client`, `DII`, `FII`, `Pro`, `TOTAL` with columns `Future Index Long`, `Future Index Short`. Compute `share = (FII_long − FII_short) / TOTAL_long`, then take the difference between two consecutive dated sessions. Center and scale differences on the previous 60 usable report pairs, excluding the feature being standardized. Skip gaps in the pair. At 10:00 IST use the latest report independently known to be available plus its predecessor. For historical development compare the prior-session join with a one-session-extra-lag sensitivity. For prospective evidence capture the actual availability on receipt. Target the near NIFTY future return from the causal 10:00 decision to 11:00. Fit one regularized linear baseline on earlier sessions with the 10:00-known opening gap, opening return and range, previous session return, expiry/roll flags and a volatility measure. Refit the same design with just the participant field added. Use chronological anchored blocks and date-level paired differences. Compare sign and magnitude beyond the opening move. Examine month-end and dated contract-mix breaks, and compare a within-regime block-shuffled or lagged feature with the actual feature. Report eligible files, late files, missing pairs, matched dates and forecast errors. If the effect disappears on matched dates, close it. If it survives, apply the frozen option admission and check both stresses. A roll-only pattern would be an exploratory finding requiring its own explanation and future test. [NSE schema](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/press/EOD_FAO.pdf) · [Clark and West on comparing nested forecasts](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~cengel/IMF/ClarkWest2007.pdf)
