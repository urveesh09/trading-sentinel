### SN412 — Measure how much of the wage is physically available

Extend SN351 from a small set of fixed entry clocks to a one-position hindsight ceiling on **each recorded day**. Within the recorded chain and permitted clocks, give the hypothetical trader the best legal one-lot entry and a later full-lot bid exit after realistic latency. Charge costs and report the ceiling with and without the modeled stop. Use actual asks, actual bids and verified lot sizes where quotes exist. Whole-day chains and the one-second opening-ATM archives have different strike coverage. Publish separate bounds for their recorded universes, mark holes unknown, and show both observed-date and all-panel denominators. For a smaller set of fixed decision clocks, also report matched buy/abstain results and how often an executable ₹750 was available before the planned stop. The ceiling tells us which portions of the gap are an instrument problem and which require better selection. It has full hindsight and stays out of portfolio P&L.

### SN414 — Trade a sector move that the NIFTY average hides

Choose one alternative index from SN413 on affordability and depth alone. On development data freeze a late-morning decision after the full opening noise window. A potential trigger is a sector futures move after subtracting its usual contemporaneous response to NIFTY, with most recorded constituent weight moving in the residual's direction. Fit the hedge ratio and normalize the residual using earlier dates only. Require NIFTY's own move to be small and Gold to be flat at decision time. Test the next 45 minutes of the sector index and a funded option on that sector. Use paired controls for the raw sector return, the same signal expressed on NIFTY, and sector entries matched on time, premium, expiry distance and spread. SN96 asked whether constituents predict NIFTY, and SN401 reused a gap signal on SENSEX. Here the outcome is the residual move in the instrument that actually moved. A residual that disappears after the full round trip closes the candidate. [NSE index methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/Methodology/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf)

### SN415 — Test a company-specific catalyst before pricing a stock option

Start with cash-price research on a prespecified small roster selected for pre-existing F&O liquidity, using timestamped exchange earnings releases. With official dissemination and provider receipt times, compare the next 30–60 minutes of market- and sector-adjusted returns after a fixed processing delay against matched non-announcement moves in the same stock. Include calls and puts symmetrically via the observed residual direction. This looks for second-stage repricing after the first response. Escalate to a one-lot stock-option paper test only if the remaining move survives a basic hurdle and dated option quotes show room. Exclude the broker's delivery-margin window, require an intraday unwind and carry an unfilled exit as unresolved. A stock option physically settles at expiry, so missing the unwind carries a different risk from a cash-settled index option. The public research is a design reference for testing reaction speed and trading frictions in India. ### SN417 — Find the partner's repeatable decision, using a blind replay

Use already permitted and de-identified observations, or get consent for new annotations. Build short clips ending before the trade decision with the original clocks and only information visible at the time. Include ordinary pass opportunities as well as entries and hide outcomes and eventual trade labels. Have the partner mark buy or pass, direction, maximum acceptable ask, invalidation and reason before revealing later frames. Compare executable outcomes at realistic latency against comparable random entries and passes. The clips create neither signals for the partner nor copied orders.
